
########## CH1 启程 · 会说话的录取通知书 ##########
--- G1.1 t: 三大时态 + 标志词（汉译英第一题永远从这里出）
  RULE: 一般现在时：经常/习惯/真理 → do/does，标志词 always, usually, often, every day, on Sundays。一般过去时：过去某时发生且结束 → did，标志词 yesterday, last week, in 2024, two years ago, just now。一般将来时：还没发生 → will do / be going to do，标志词 tomorrow, next week, soon, in + 时间段（in ten months）。
  EX: He usually reviews words every day, applied for the exam last month, and will take it in ten months.
  EXCN: 他通常每天复习单词，上个月报了名，十个月后参加考试。
  SIGNAL: 一句话三个空：every day → does；last month → did；in ten months → will do。标志词 = 时间的身份证
--- G1.2 t: 现在完成时 have/has done（2024 真题单选+完形双考）
  RULE: 过去发生，对现在有影响或持续到现在 → have/has + 过去分词。三大标志：① since + 过去时间点（since 2019）/ for + 时间段（for five years）② already/yet/just/ever/never ③ in the last few years / so far / recently（最近几年以来）。
  EX: The prices of houses in this area have increased in the last few years.
  EXCN: 这个地区的房价在最近几年里涨了很多。（2024 真题原句型）
  SIGNAL: 看到 in the last few years / so far 秒选现在完成；since 2019 vs for five years 一个接点一个接段，改错最爱对调它们
--- G1.3 t: It 句型三剑客（汉译英写作双高频）
  RULE: ① It takes/took sb. + 时间 + to do sth.：做某事花某人多少时间。② It's time (for sb.) to do sth.：到做某事的时间了。③ It is + adj. + (for/of sb.) + to do sth.：对某人来说做某事怎么样——形式主语 it，真正主语是 to do。
  EX: It took me more than a year to learn to draw a horse in five minutes. It is important for students to review on time.
  EXCN: 学会在五分钟内画一匹马花了我一年多。对学生来说按时复习很重要。
  SIGNAL: 句子开头是 It，后面找不到它指代的东西 → 八成是形式主语，真正主语在后面 to do 里；改错题常把 to do 换成 doing 考你
--- Q1.1: Smoking seriously ______ your health, so give it up.
  OPTS: [0]effect | [1]affects | [2]efforts | [3]effective
  ANSWER(1): affects
  EXPLAIN: 空前有副词 seriously，空后是名词，缺谓语动词 → affect。effect 是名词，efforts 是努力，effective 是形容词。
--- Q1.2: More than 300 students will ______ the job interview tomorrow morning.
  OPTS: [0]join | [1]attend | [2]take part | [3]acquire
  ANSWER(1): attend
  EXPLAIN: interview 面试属于到场性质的场合 → attend。join 接组织或人，take part in 才是参加活动，acquire 是获得。
--- Q1.3: The ______ for handing in your assignment is next Friday.
  OPTS: [0]deadline | [1]campus | [2]degree | [3]attendance
  ANSWER(0): deadline
  EXPLAIN: 交作业的最后期限 = deadline。校园生活高频词，完形改错都爱考。
--- Q1.4: She has been studying English ______ she was five years old.
  OPTS: [0]for | [1]since | [2]from | [3]in
  ANSWER(1): since
  EXPLAIN: since + 过去时间点（she was five），for + 时间段。2024 真题原题：has been studying...since...，现在完成时标志词。
--- Q1.5: It ______ me two hours to review the notes every night.
  OPTS: [0]spends | [1]pays | [2]takes | [3]costs
  ANSWER(2): takes
  EXPLAIN: It takes sb. + 时间 + to do 固定句型，it 作形式主语。spend 主语必须是人，cost 主语是物，pay 主语是人且花钱。
--- Q1.6: In China, most students live ______ campus during the school term.
  OPTS: [0]in the | [1]at a | [2]on | [3]into
  ANSWER(2): on
  EXPLAIN: 固定搭配 on campus 在校园里，中间不加冠词。介词搭配是完形填空的第一大考点。
--- Q1.7: 改错：Last week he applies for the exam, but he missed the deadline.
  OPTS: [0]applies → applied | [1]but → and | [2]missed → misses | [3]for → to
  ANSWER(0): applies → applied
  EXPLAIN: 改错雷区①时态不一致：last week 是过去时标志词，but 连接的前后谓语时态要一致 → applied。后半句 missed 已经是过去时。
--- Q1.8: 英译汉选择：Hard work will benefit you in the long run.
  OPTS: [0]努力会让你在长跑中喘不过气。 | [1]努力终将使你长期受益。 | [2]努力会在长期的奔跑中帮助你。 | [3]努力让你受益于长跑。
  ANSWER(1): 努力终将使你长期受益。
  EXPLAIN: benefit sb. 使某人受益（及物用法）；in the long run 固定搭配「从长远来看」。望文生义是英译汉选择的最大陷阱。
--- S1.1: What many students fail to realize is that the <mark>effort</mark> they make in the first year will <mark>affect</mark> their chances of winning a <mark>scholarship</mark>.
  PART: 主干：What many students fail to realize is + 表语从句（主系表结构）
  PART: What 引导主语从句 = 「……的东西」，很多学生没能意识到的东西
  PART: 表语从句里套了个定语从句：the effort (that) they make 他们付出的努力
  PART: affect 是动词「影响」——如果你背成了 effect，这句直接读崩
--- S1.2: Only when you <mark>concentrate</mark> on what you are learning can knowledge truly become your own.
  PART: Only + 状语从句放在句首 → 主句必须部分倒装：can knowledge become
  PART: 正常语序：knowledge can truly become your own
  PART: concentrate on 固定搭配「专注于」；what you are learning 是介词 on 的宾语从句
  PART: 看懂倒装的信号：句首 Only / Never / Not until，后面找 be/情态动词提前

########## CH2 朝夕 · 宿舍四兄弟与破产危机 ##########
--- G2.1 t: 冠词 a / an / the（音素决定 a/an，改错第一雷区）
  RULE: a/an 看【音素】不看字母：元音音素开头用 an（an hour, an honest man），辅音音素开头用 a（a university, a useful book，u 发 /juː/）。the 表特指：二次提及、世上独一无二、the same 固定搭配。特例：go to school / go to bed / at school 表「去做这件事」时不加 the，go to the school 才是去那栋楼。
  EX: She is an honest girl. She spent an hour and a half on a useful app, and now she keeps the same schedule every day.
  EXCN: 她是个诚实的女孩。她花了一个半小时在一个有用的应用上，现在每天保持着同样的时间表。
  SIGNAL: 改错高频：a hour ❌ / an university ❌ / same 前丢 the ❌；go to school 特例不加 the
--- G2.2 t: 频率副词与一般现在时（主谓一致钉子户）
  RULE: always / usually / often / sometimes / seldom / hardly ever / never 是频率副词，出现即提示一般现在时。位置口诀：be 后实前——be 动词之后（He is always late），实义动词之前（He always stays up）。主语是第三人称单数，动词必须加 s/es。
  EX: My roommate never cleans the floor, but he is always ready to complain about the noise.
  EXCN: 我室友从不打扫地板，但他总是随时准备抱怨噪音。
  SIGNAL: never + cleans（实义动词前）；is + always + ready（be 动词后）；改错高频：He always go ❌ → goes
--- G2.3 t: spend / cost / pay / take 四花费（完形单选双料冠军）
  RULE: 人 + spend + 时间/钱 + on sth. / (in) doing sth.；人 + pay + 钱 + for sth.；物 + cost + 人 + 钱（不用于时间）；it + take + 人 + 时间 + to do sth.。口诀：人 spend 人 pay，物 cost，it takes 人 to do。
  EX: I spent 999 yuan on that device, and it cost me two evenings as well. It took me one night to realize that I had paid for a talking alarm clock.
  EXCN: 我花 999 元买了那台设备，还搭进去两个晚上。我花了一晚上才明白：我买的是一个会说话的闹钟。
  SIGNAL: cost 的过去式还是 cost；spend 的过去式 spent；四选一先看主语是人、物还是 it
--- Q2.1: His monthly ______ is only 800 yuan, so he writes down every expense to control his spending.
  OPTS: [0]budget | [1]balance | [2]schedule | [3]device
  ANSWER(0): budget
  EXPLAIN: 每月只有 800 还要控制开销 → budget 预算。后文 expense（开销）是提示词。balance 平衡，schedule 时间表，device 设备，都没有「额度」含义。
--- Q2.2: This little ______ can control the lights, the alarm and even the rice cooker in our dormitory.
  OPTS: [0]effort | [1]device | [2]deadline | [3]account
  ANSWER(1): device
  EXPLAIN: 能控制电灯、闹铃和电饭煲的东西 → device 设备。2024 真题完形考过 mobile device（移动设备）。
--- Q2.3: Could you ______ me your notes? I will return them before the deadline.
  OPTS: [0]borrow | [1]lend | [2]rent | [3]keep
  ANSWER(1): lend
  EXPLAIN: 借给别人用 lend：lend sb. sth.。borrow 是借入，且不能说 borrow sb. sth.（改错高频错误），rent 是付租金的租。
--- Q2.4: The camera ______ him 2,000 yuan last month.
  OPTS: [0]spent | [1]paid | [2]cost | [3]took
  ANSWER(2): cost
  EXPLAIN: 物作主语用 cost：The camera cost him 2,000 yuan。spend/pay 主语必须是人，take 用于 it takes sb. time to do 句型。
--- Q2.5: Keeping a budget is ______ useful habit, not a punishment.
  OPTS: [0]a | [1]an | [2]the | [3]不填
  ANSWER(0): a
  EXPLAIN: useful 以辅音音素 /j/ 开头（u 虽是元音字母），所以用 a。an 只用于元音音素开头：an hour, an honest man。
--- Q2.6: 改错：My roommate always stay up late playing games, so his grades are terrible.
  OPTS: [0]stay → stays | [1]always → never | [2]playing → play | [3]are → is
  ANSWER(0): stay → stays
  EXPLAIN: 改错雷区②主谓一致：always 提示一般现在时，my roommate 是第三人称单数 → stays。playing 作伴随状语没错，grades 复数配 are 也没错。
--- Q2.7: 英译汉选择：After buying the Word Master 3000, Qian-shao is in the red this month.
  OPTS: [0]钱少这个月红光满面。 | [1]钱少这个月负债了。 | [2]钱少这个月穿红色衣服。 | [3]钱少这个月大发雷霆。
  ANSWER(1): 钱少这个月负债了。
  EXPLAIN: in the red = 负债、出现赤字（账本用红字记亏损），与红衣、脸色无关；反义搭配 in the black = 盈利。望文生义是英译汉最大陷阱。
--- Q2.8: Don't waste your limited budget ______ those useless things, or your wallet will cry again.
  OPTS: [0]on | [1]for | [2]in | [3]to
  ANSWER(0): on
  EXPLAIN: waste time/money on sth. 固定搭配，介词 on。同族搭配 spend...on。介词搭配是完形第一大考点。
--- S2.1: When it comes to <mark>managing</mark> money, what matters most is not how much you earn but how you spend it.
  PART: when it comes to + 名词/doing = 「说到……的时候」，to 是介词
  PART: what matters most 是主语从句（最重要的东西）
  PART: not...but... 平行结构：不是你挣多少，而是你怎么花
  PART: earn 和 spend 前面都有 how，是两个并列的宾语从句
--- S2.2: Many students <mark>spend</mark> hours playing games, <mark>which</mark> makes it impossible for them to finish their homework on time.
  PART: spend + 时间 + doing：spend hours playing（花几小时打游戏）
  PART: which 引导非限制性定语从句，指代前面整件事
  PART: makes it impossible... 中的 it 是形式宾语，真正的宾语是 to finish...
  PART: on time = 准时；in time = 及时，别混

########## CH3 试炼 · 面试官是个狠角色 ##########
--- G3.1 t: 可数与不可数名词（职场面试第一大坑）
  RULE: 不可数名词没有复数、不能加 a/an、不能被 many/few 修饰：advice 建议、information 信息、news 消息、equipment 设备、experience 经验、knowledge 知识、progress 进步、furniture 家具。要表示数量就借单位：a piece of advice 一条建议、two pieces of information 两条信息。experience 当「经历」讲可数（a wonderful experience），当「经验」讲不可数——一词两性是真题最爱。
  EX: The interviewer gave me some useful advice, and the company bought two pieces of equipment for each new employee.
  EXCN: 面试官给了我一些有用的建议，公司为每个新员工买了两件设备。
  SIGNAL: 见到 advice/information/news/equipment/experience（经验）/knowledge/progress：不能加 s、不能配 many/a/an；改错题最爱把 progresses、advices 挖出来
--- G3.2 t: 名词所有格 's 与 of 结构
  RULE: 有生命（人或动物）用 's：the candidate's resume 候选人的简历；无生命用 of：the door of the office 办公室的门。时间、距离也可用 's：ten minutes' walk 十分钟的路、two weeks' notice。双重所有格：a friend of my father's = 我父亲朋友中的一位。's 所有格后接的名词常可省略（他的办公室 = his，不是 his one）。
  EX: This is the manager's office, and the walls of the office are covered with other candidates' resumes.
  EXCN: 这是经理的办公室，办公室的墙上贴满了其他候选人的简历。
  SIGNAL: 一句话两种所有格同框：manager's（人）与 of the office（物）；注意 candidates' 是复数所有格，撇号在 s 后；改错爱考 ten minutes walk ❌ → ten minutes' walk
--- G3.3 t: many / much / few / little 修饰语矩阵
  RULE: many / few / a few + 可数名词复数：many candidates 候选人多，few candidates 几乎没人，a few candidates 有几个。much / little / a little + 不可数名词：much time 时间多，little time 几乎没时间，a little time 有一点时间。有 a 是肯定（还有一点），没 a 是否定（几乎没有）。quite a few = 相当多，别被 quite 骗了。
  EX: He has little work experience but makes few mistakes, so he saves much time and avoids many problems.
  EXCN: 他几乎没什么工作经验，却几乎不犯错，所以他省下大量时间、避开很多问题。
  SIGNAL: 先判断名词可数不可数，再看有没有 a：little time 时间不够 vs a little time 还来得及——一字之差，面试生死之别
--- Q3.1: The company decided to ______ ten new employees this year.
  OPTS: [0]fire | [1]hire | [2]quit | [3]rent
  ANSWER(1): hire
  EXPLAIN: hire new employees 雇新员工。fire 是解雇，quit 是辞职，rent 是租。hire 与 fire 一进一出，扫爷的口头禅。
--- Q3.2: Workers who are paid by the hour receive a weekly ______.
  OPTS: [0]salary | [1]wage | [2]income | [3]budget
  ANSWER(1): wage
  EXPLAIN: 按小时/周计酬的是 wage；salary 是月薪年薪；income 是总收入；budget 是预算。口诀：salary 按月，wage 按时。
--- Q3.3: The ______ is the person who conducts the interview and asks the questions.
  OPTS: [0]interviewee | [1]interviewer | [2]employee | [3]candidate
  ANSWER(1): interviewer
  EXPLAIN: interviewer 面试官（提问的人），interviewee 应试者（被问的人）。-er 主动，-ee 被动，与 employer/employee 同一套规则。
--- Q3.4: The interviewer gave me some valuable ______ on how to improve my resume.
  OPTS: [0]advices | [1]advice | [2]suggestions of advice | [3]pieces of advices
  ANSWER(1): advice
  EXPLAIN: advice 是不可数名词，不能加 s；a piece of advice 才是一条建议。suggestion 可数，但此空前是 some，只有 advice 完全正确。
--- Q3.5: — Whose office is this? — It is ______.
  OPTS: [0]the manager | [1]the manager's | [2]of the manager | [3]the managers
  ANSWER(1): the manager's
  EXPLAIN: 问谁的 → 用名词所有格 's：the manager's（office），撇号 + s 表所属。the manager 是人本身，of the manager 语序错，the managers 是复数无所有格。
--- Q3.6: 改错：She has little experiences in this field, so she works harder than anyone else.
  OPTS: [0]little → a little | [1]experiences → experience | [2]harder → hard | [3]anyone → everyone
  ANSWER(1): experiences → experience
  EXPLAIN: 改错雷区③名词单复数：experience 作「经验」讲不可数，不能加 s。little 本身没错——没经验所以更努力，逻辑成立。
--- Q3.7: 英译汉选择：He is a green hand in this company, so he often asks his colleagues for help.
  OPTS: [0]他是公司里的绿手套，所以经常向同事求助。 | [1]他是公司里的新手，所以经常向同事求助。 | [2]他是公司里的环保使者，所以经常向同事求助。 | [3]他是公司里负责绿植的，所以经常向同事求助。
  ANSWER(1): 他是公司里的新手，所以经常向同事求助。
  EXPLAIN: green hand 是习语「新手、生手」，源自园艺新手上沾的绿，不能望文生义翻成绿手套。colleague 同事，职场阅读高频词。
--- Q3.8: A good employee should be able to work well ______ pressure.
  OPTS: [0]under | [1]on | [2]in | [3]at
  ANSWER(0): under
  EXPLAIN: under pressure 在压力下，固定介词搭配，阿洛面试的原话考点。改错题常把 under 挖掉换成 in。
--- S3.1: <mark>What</mark> employers value most is not the diploma itself but the ability to <mark>solve real problems</mark>.
  PART: what employers value most 是主语从句：雇主最看重的东西
  PART: not...but... 平行结构：不是文凭本身，而是解决问题的能力
  PART: the ability to do sth. 做某事的能力，不定式作后置定语
  PART: diploma 文凭，职场阅读高频词
--- S3.2: It was his three years of work experience that <mark>helped him stand out</mark> among hundreds of candidates.
  PART: It was...that... 强调句：被强调部分是 his three years of work experience
  PART: 判断方法：去掉 It was 和 that，句子仍然完整 → 强调句
  PART: stand out 脱颖而出；among hundreds of candidates 在数百名候选人中
  PART: experience 作「经验」讲是不可数名词，没有 s——本章语法①的主角

########## CH4 科技时代 · 智能机器人兄弟 ##########
--- G4.1 t: it 用法：形式主语 + It is...that 强调句初识
  RULE: ① 形式主语：It is + adj./n. + (for/of sb.) + to do...——it 只占主语的位置，真正的主语是后面的不定式；It takes sb. + 时间/钱 + to do 也算这一家。② 强调句初识：It is/was + 被强调部分 + that/who + 其余部分，用来突出「正是它」。判断法：去掉 It is...that 后剩下的话还能还原成完整句子，就是强调句。
  EX: It is necessary for every device to update its system regularly. It was Sao-Ye that fixed the Wi-Fi last night.
  EXCN: 每台设备定期更新系统很有必要。昨晚正是扫爷修好的 Wi-Fi。
  SIGNAL: 句首是 It，往后找不到它指代的东西 → 八成是形式主语；强调句去掉 It is...that 句子依然完整，否则就不是强调句——单选最爱考这个辨析
--- G4.2 t: one / it / that / those 指代区别（比较级句型钉子户）
  RULE: it 指前文提到的同一个东西（特指，同一台）；one 指同类不同个（泛指可数单数，one = a + 单数名词）；that 指代前文的可数单数或不可数名词（= the + 名词，比较级句型高频）；those 是 that 的复数（= the + 复数名词）。
  EX: The Wi-Fi in Dorm 302 is faster than that in the library. The robots made in 2025 run faster than those made in 2010. My phone is broken, so I will buy a new one.
  EXCN: 302 宿舍的 Wi-Fi 比图书馆的快。2025 年造的机器人比 2010 年的那些跑得快。我的手机坏了，所以我要买个新的（同类的另一台）。
  SIGNAL: 比较级挖空题：不可数名词 → that；可数复数 → those；泛指可数单数 → one；同一个东西 → it。2024 真题完形考过 that 指代 the air/weather 这类不可数
--- G4.3 t: 物主代词 + 反身代词（enjoy oneself 改错雷区）

########## CH5 身心修炼 · 健身房办卡风云 ##########
--- G5.1 t: 形容词 ↔ 副词：转换规则与位置（改错雷区⑤词性）
  RULE: 形容词修饰名词、作系动词表语；副词修饰实义动词、形容词、副词或整个句子。大部分形容词 + ly 变副词：careful→carefully，regular→regularly；特殊：good→well，hard 本身就是副词（hardly = 几乎不，意思全变）。位置：修饰动词的副词可放动词前后或句尾；enough 修饰形容词要后置（strong enough）。系动词 look/sound/feel/taste 后面用形容词不用副词。
  EX: He is a careful coach, and he always watches the new members carefully. The training plan sounds good.
  EXCN: 他是个细心的教练，总是细心地观察新会员。这份训练计划听起来不错。
  SIGNAL: 改错口诀：实义动词前后找 ly，系动词后用原形。hard/hardly、late/lately、near/nearly 三对真假兄弟是完形最爱
--- G5.2 t: 比较级与最高级：much/even 修饰 + fewer/less
  RULE: 比较级 + than；比较级前可用 much / even / far / a lot / a little 加强（very 不能修饰比较级！）。the + 比较级 = 两者中「较……的那个」；the more..., the more... 越……越……。可数名词复数用 fewer，不可数名词用 less。最高级：the + est/most，范围用 in（地点）/ of（人或物）。
  EX: After one month, A-Luo is much stronger and makes far fewer mistakes. Health is the most important thing of all.
  EXCN: 一个月后，阿洛壮实多了，犯的错也少得多。健康是所有事情中最重要的。
  SIGNAL: 看见 than 或空前 much/even → 比较级；fewer/less 看后面名词可不可数；very + 比较级 = 必错
--- G5.3 t: so...that 与 such...that（结果状语从句）
  RULE: so + 形容词/副词 + that 从句：如此……以至于……；such + (a/an) + 形容词 + 名词 + that 从句。口诀：so 修饰形副，such 修饰名词短语。特例：名词前有 many/much/few/little 时必须用 so（so many people / so little time）。so + adj + a/an + 单数名词 = such a/an + adj + 单数名词（so hot a day = such a hot day）。
  EX: The training was so hard that A-Luo slept like a log. It was such a hard month that he almost gave up.
  EXCN: 训练太苦，阿洛睡得像根木头。那是如此艰难的一个月，他差点放弃。
  SIGNAL: 空后面直接是名词短语 → such；空后面是单个 adj/adv → so；看到 many/much/few/little 无脑选 so
--- Q5.1: The doctor was very ______ with his ______, explaining everything slowly.
  OPTS: [0]patient; patients | [1]patient; patience | [2]patience; patient | [3]patiently; patients
  ANSWER(0): patient; patients
  EXPLAIN: be patient with sb. 对某人有耐心；patients 病人复数。一词两义高频考点。
--- Q5.2: Regular exercise helps him ______ a healthy weight and a clear mind.
  OPTS: [0]prevent | [1]maintain | [2]suffer | [3]waste
  ANSWER(1): maintain
  EXPLAIN: maintain 保持、维持，真题高频词（maintain order / maintain a balance）。prevent 预防、阻止（prevent...from）；suffer 遭受；waste 浪费。
--- Q5.3: Eating too much salt and fried food may lead to heart ______.
  OPTS: [0]disease | [1]illness | [2]sick | [3]medicine
  ANSWER(0): disease
  EXPLAIN: heart disease 固定搭配；disease 指有名字的具体病种。illness 偏指生病的状态，sick 是形容词，medicine 是药。
--- Q5.4: After one month of training, A-Luo felt ______ stronger than before.
  OPTS: [0]very | [1]much | [2]more | [3]most
  ANSWER(1): much
  EXPLAIN: much / even / far / a lot 可以修饰比较级；very 只能修饰原级（very strong ✔，very stronger ✘）。more stronger 双重比较级是改错雷区。
--- Q5.5: It was ______ hard training that A-Luo slept like a log that night.
  OPTS: [0]so | [1]such | [2]so a | [3]such a
  ANSWER(1): such
  EXPLAIN: such + (a/an) + adj + 名词；training 是不可数名词，不能加 a → such hard training。so 后面直接跟 adj/adv：so hard。注意 so many / so much / so few / so little 是固定特例。
--- Q5.6: 改错：The coach watched him careful and shook his head.
  OPTS: [0]careful → carefully | [1]watched → watch | [2]and → but | [3]shook → shake
  ANSWER(0): careful → carefully
  EXPLAIN: 改错雷区⑤词性：careful 修饰实义动词 watched，必须用副词 carefully。and 前后时态一致 ✔，主语 coach 单数、shook 过去式 ✔。
--- Q5.7: 英译汉选择：The terrible check-up report was a wake-up call for A-Luo.
  OPTS: [0]这份可怕的体检报告是一通叫阿洛起床的电话。 | [1]这份可怕的体检报告给阿洛敲响了警钟。 | [2]这份可怕的体检报告是阿洛的起床闹铃。 | [3]这份可怕的体检报告把阿洛叫醒了三次。

  OPTS: [0]of | [1]with | [2]to | [3]in
  ANSWER(2): to
  EXPLAIN: be addicted to 沉迷于，to 是介词，后面接名词或 doing。改错高频：be addicted to drink ✘ → to drinking ✔。
--- S5.1: Regular exercise not only builds up your body but also helps you <mark>reduce stress</mark> and keep a healthy mind.
  PART: not only...but also 平行结构：前后都是动词短语，必须对等（改错高频）

  PART: What many young people don't realize 是主语从句：年轻人没有意识到的东西

########## CH6 环境保护 · 绿色森林保卫战 ##########
--- G6.1 t: 并列连词 and/but/or/so；although 不与 but 连用（改错雷区⑨）
  RULE: and 并列顺承；but 转折；or 或者/否则（祈使句 + or = 否则就……）；so 因果结果。⚠️ 一个句子里 although 和 but 只能留一个，because 和 so 同罪——连词只能用一次，中文「虽然……但是……」的直译是最大的坑。
  EX: Although the White Beast looked strong, it was made of bags. The valley was dirty, so we cleaned it up.
  EXCN: 虽然白色巨兽看起来很强，但它是袋子做的。山谷很脏，所以我们把它清理干净了。
  SIGNAL: 改错看到 Although...but 连用 → 删其一；Because...so 连用 → 同罪。or 在祈使句后 = 否则：Hurry up, or you will miss it.
--- G6.2 t: because vs because of（单选改错双高频）
  RULE: because + 完整句子（有自己的主谓）；because of + 名词/代词/doing（短语）。同义替换：because of = due to + 名词。判断口诀：空后有主谓选 because，只有名词短语选 because of。
  EX: We stayed at home because it rained heavily. = We stayed at home because of the heavy rain.
  EXCN: 因为雨下得大，我们待在家里。
  SIGNAL: 改错爱把 because of 后面塞进一个句子 → 删 of；或 because 后面只跟了名词 → 补 of
--- G6.3 t: 时间状语从句 when/while/as soon as/before（主将从现）
  RULE: when 通吃（短暂、延续动作都行）；while 只接延续动作，常配进行时；as soon as 一……就……；before 在……之前。铁律：主句将来时，从句用一般现在时（主将从现）——When the beast comes 才对，will come 是错的。
  EX: As soon as the rain stopped, we went on planting trees. While the beast was sleeping, we picked up the bags.
  EXCN: 雨一停我们就继续种树。趁巨兽睡觉时，我们把袋子捡走了。
  SIGNAL: while 后接短暂动词（While he came ❌）→ 换 when；主将从现：When/As soon as/If + 一般现在时，主句才用 will
--- Q6.1: The earth's ______ is becoming warmer and warmer, which is a serious problem for all living things.
  OPTS: [0]atmosphere | [1]appointment | [2]attraction | [3]application
  ANSWER(0): atmosphere
  EXPLAIN: 大气层变暖是全球变暖语境 → atmosphere。其余是形近词干扰：appointment 预约、attraction 吸引力、application 申请。

  ANSWER(1): resources
  EXPLAIN: natural resources 自然资源固定搭配。sources 是来源（信息来源），courses 课程，senses 感官。resource 是能用完的储备，source 是从哪来。
--- Q6.3: The sun ______ in the east and sets in the west.
  OPTS: [0]raises | [1]rises | [2]raise | [3]arise
  ANSWER(1): rises
  EXPLAIN: 太阳自己升起 → 不及物 rise，三单 rises。raise 是及物动词要接宾语（raise your hand），arise 指问题出现。改错钉子户：The sun raises ❌。

  OPTS: [0]Because | [1]Because of | [2]Since of | [3]As for
  ANSWER(1): Because of

--- Q6.5: The valley was full of rubbish, ______ the young volunteers spent the whole morning cleaning it up.
  OPTS: [0]or | [1]but | [2]so | [3]although
  ANSWER(2): so
  EXPLAIN: 前因后果 → so。or 或者/否则，but 转折（前后语义矛盾才用），although 尽管（且不能与 but 连用）。逻辑连词题先判断两句关系再选。

--- Q6.7: 英译汉选择：Trees take in carbon dioxide and give out oxygen, so people call them the lungs of the earth.
  OPTS: [0]树把二氧化碳拿进屋里，又把氧气分出去，所以人们管树叫地球的空气。 | [1]树吸收二氧化碳并释放氧气，因此人们把树木称为地球之肺。 | [2]树接受二氧化碳的礼物又放弃氧气，所以地球的肺在呼吸。 | [3]树把二氧化碳收进口袋再吐出氧气，因此地球的肺需要锻炼。

  EXPLAIN: 椅子看得出木头原材料 → of；纸看不出木头 → from。看得出用 of，看不出用 from；be made in 后接产地，三个介词改错轮着考。
--- S6.1: It is everyone's duty to <mark>protect</mark> the environment, because the resources on the earth are <mark>running out</mark> faster than we expected.

  PART: if 条件状语从句口诀「主将从现」：从句用 takes 现在时，主句用 will 将来时（改错高频）
  PART: take action = take measures / steps 采取行动
  PART: will be greatly reduced 一般将来时被动语态：将被大大减少

########## CH7 市井烟火 · 双十一购物车大作战 ##########
--- G7.1 t: V + doing（enjoy / finish / practice / mind / suggest 后接动名词）
  RULE: 一批动词后面只能接 doing，不能接 to do：enjoy doing 喜欢做、finish doing 做完、practice doing 练习做、mind doing 介意做、suggest doing 建议做，外加 keep / avoid / miss。改错年年挖坑：enjoy to play ❌ → enjoy playing ✓。
  EX: Would you mind giving me a refund? He suggested comparing prices before purchasing.
  EXCN: 你介意给我退款吗？他建议购买之前先比比价。
  SIGNAL: 看到 enjoy/finish/practice/mind/suggest 后面的空 → 秒选 -ing；改错看到 enjoy to do / suggest to do 直接改 doing
--- G7.2 t: V + to do（want / decide / hope / refuse / afford 后接不定式）
  RULE: 另一批动词后面只能接 to do：want to do 想做、decide to do 决定做、hope to do 希望做、refuse to do 拒绝做、can't afford to do 买不起/承担不起，外加 plan / promise / agree。afford 前面通常要有 can/can't/be able to 帮忙。
  EX: He decided to return the lamp and hoped to get a full refund, but the shop refused to reply.
  EXCN: 他决定退掉那盏灯并希望拿到全额退款，但商店拒绝回复。
  SIGNAL: 两大家族别串门：decide doing ❌ → decide to do ✓；enjoy to sing ❌ → enjoy singing ✓；看空格前的动词是谁，再决定接 to do 还是 doing
--- G7.3 t: make / let / have sb do 使役动词 + 原形
  RULE: 使役三兄弟 make / let / have 后面接宾语 + 动词原形（不带 to）：make sb do 逼某人做、let sb do 允许某人做、have sb do 让某人做。Ads make people believe... 被动语态时 to 要还回来：be made to do（下一章见）。
  EX: The ads made him believe the lamp was a bargain, and the coupon let him save ten yuan.
  EXCN: 广告让他相信这盏灯很划算，优惠券让他省了十块钱。
  SIGNAL: 改错高频：make sb to do ❌ → make sb do ✓；再区分：make sb do（使役+原形）vs make sb happy（使役+形容词）
--- Q7.1: If what you bought has quality problems within seven days, you can ask for a full ______.
  OPTS: [0]refund | [1]reward | [2]review | [3]respect
  ANSWER(0): refund
  EXPLAIN: 七天无理由退货的退款 = ask for a full refund。reward 是报酬，review 是评论/复习，respect 是尊重。
--- Q7.2: The lamp was cheap, but its ______ was terrible — it broke in two days.
  OPTS: [0]quantity | [1]quality | [2]discount | [3]bargain
  ANSWER(1): quality
  EXPLAIN: 质量 = quality，of poor quality 质量差。quantity 是数量，音近陷阱；discount 是折扣，bargain 是便宜货。
--- Q7.3: Jane won the first ______ in the English speech contest.
  OPTS: [0]price | [1]prize | [2]praise | [3]pride
  ANSWER(1): prize
  EXPLAIN: win the first prize 获一等奖。price 价格、praise 表扬、pride 自豪。改错钉子户，见易混组。
--- Q7.4: My roommate suggested ______ the reviews carefully before placing an order.
  OPTS: [0]to read | [1]reading | [2]read | [3]to be read
  ANSWER(1): reading
  EXPLAIN: suggest + doing（语法①）：suggest / enjoy / finish / practice / mind 同族动词后接动名词。to read 是 V+to do 家族设下的陷阱。
--- Q7.5: After the painful lesson, Qian-Shao decided ______ a shopping list before every Double Eleven.
  OPTS: [0]making | [1]to make | [2]make | [3]made
  ANSWER(1): to make
  EXPLAIN: decide + to do（语法②）：want / hope / refuse / afford 同族。making 是 V+doing 家族设下的陷阱。
--- Q7.6: 改错：The advertisement made me to believe that the cheap lamp was a bargain.
  OPTS: [0]to believe → believe | [1]made → makes | [2]believe → believed | [3]that → what
  ANSWER(0): to believe → believe
  EXPLAIN: make sb do sth 使役动词后接动词原形（语法③），to 必须删掉，属改错雷区⑦非谓语。make 后面也可直接接形容词：make me happy。
--- Q7.7: 英译汉选择：The lamp looked like a bargain, but it turned out to be a white elephant.
  OPTS: [0]这盏灯看起来像一笔划算买卖，结果却是一头白色的大象。 | [1]这盏灯看起来便宜，结果却是个昂贵又没用的累赘。 | [2]这盏灯看起来像白象，结果真的变成了大象。 | [3]这盏灯看起来划算，所以大家抢着买。
  ANSWER(1): 这盏灯看起来便宜，结果却是个昂贵又没用的累赘。
  EXPLAIN: white elephant = 昂贵又无用、留着累赘的东西，不是白色大象；bargain 此处是「便宜货/划算的交易」，不是讨价还价。望文生义是英译汉第一大陷阱。
--- Q7.8: During Double Eleven, almost everything on this app is ______ a discount.
  OPTS: [0]at | [1]on | [2]in | [3]for
  ANSWER(0): at
  EXPLAIN: at a discount 固定搭配，介词用 at。别被 on sale 带偏：sale 前用 on，discount 前用 at。中英折扣方向相反：30% discount = 七折。
--- S7.1: With so many products on sale, <mark>consumers</mark> should <mark>compare</mark> the quality and prices before making a <mark>choice</mark>.
  PART: With so many products on sale 是 with 复合结构「有这么多商品在打折」
  PART: on sale 在打折出售（for sale 是待售，不一定打折）
  PART: before making a choice：before 介词 + 动名词
  PART: make a choice = choose 的名词化表达
--- S7.2: What <mark>advertisements</mark> want you to believe is that buying more will make you happier, but the fact is quite different.
  PART: What advertisements want you to believe 是主语从句：广告想让你相信的东西
  PART: is that... 表语从句，说明「相信」的内容
  PART: make you happier：make + 宾语 + 形容词「让某人……」，使役动词后不接 to do
  PART: but 转折后是作者真实观点——阅读题态度题常问转折后面的内容

########## CH8 挂科逆袭 · 暴风雨之夜 ##########
--- G8.1 t: 被动语态 be done 各时态（本章主菜）
  RULE: 被动 = be + 过去分词，时态全靠变 be：一般现在 am/is/are done（English is spoken here）；一般过去 was/were done（The power was cut off last night）；一般将来 will be done（The resit will be held in two weeks）；现在完成 have/has been done（The results have been posted）。主语是动作的承受者，真正干活的人用 by 引出。
  EX: The midterm results were posted last night, and the make-up exam will be held in two weeks.
  EXCN: 期中成绩昨晚被公布，补考将在两周后举行。
  SIGNAL: 看到主语是物/动作承受者、或不知道谁干的 → 被动。改错坑：漏 be（The bridge built in 1890 ❌ 缺谓语）
--- G8.2 t: 无被动动词四兄弟：take place / happen / consist of / come true
  RULE: 这四个不及物动词短语后面不能接宾语，也就永远没有被动语态：was happened ❌、was taken place ❌、is consisted of ❌、was come true ❌，全部判错。同族还有 break out（爆发）、belong to（属于）。
  EX: Great changes have taken place in my hometown, and my dream has come true at last.
  EXCN: 我的家乡发生了巨大变化，我的梦想最终实现了。
  SIGNAL: 改错最爱：看到这四个词带了 been/was done 形式直接划掉被动部分；happen 主语是事情，take place 主语是活动/变化
--- G8.3 t: 情态动词 + be done（被动与情态的合体技）
  RULE: 情态动词后必须接动词原形，要表被动就用 be done：must be finished（必须被完成）、can be seen（能被看见）、should be handed in（应该被上交）、may be prevented（可能被阻止）。否定词 not 放在情态动词后面：will not be accepted。
  EX: The assignment must be handed in before the deadline, or it will not be accepted.
  EXCN: 作业必须在截止日期前上交，否则将不被接受。
  SIGNAL: 情态动词后面直接跟过去分词（must finished ❌）或跟了 to do（must to be finished ❌）都是错的——中间必须隔着 be
--- Q8.1: The radio says a heavy ______ is on its way tonight. Close the windows before you leave.
  OPTS: [0]storm | [1]sunshine | [2]fog | [3]survey
  ANSWER(0): storm
  EXPLAIN: a heavy storm 一场大暴风雨——本章主题词。sunshine 阳光、fog 雾、survey 调查，跟 close the windows 的警告语境不搭。
--- Q8.2: The ______ was cut off for three hours during the storm, so we studied by candlelight.
  OPTS: [0]power | [1]pressure | [2]energy | [3]strength
  ANSWER(0): power
  EXPLAIN: power cut / the power was cut off 停电，固定表达。pressure 压力、energy 精力、strength 力量——三个近义干扰项都是词义辨析坑。
--- Q8.3: After two weeks of hard work, Qian-Shao finally ______ in passing the make-up exam.
  OPTS: [0]success | [1]succeeded | [2]successful | [3]successfully
  ANSWER(1): succeeded
  EXPLAIN: finally 后缺谓语动词 → succeed in doing 成功做成某事。in 是介词，接 doing 不接 to do——success 全家桶第一钉子户。
--- Q8.4: The assignment must ______ before next Friday, or the teacher will not accept it.
  OPTS: [0]hand in | [1]be handed in | [2]be handed | [3]hand
  ANSWER(1): be handed in
  EXPLAIN: 情态动词后接动词原形，作业是「被上交」→ must be handed in（情态 + be + done）。hand in 的 in 是小品词不能丢，所以 be handed ❌。
--- Q8.5: The old tower ______ in 1890, but it is still standing after the storm.
  OPTS: [0]built | [1]was built | [2]is built | [3]has built
  ANSWER(1): was built
  EXPLAIN: tower 是「被建造」的承受者 → 被动语态；in 1890 是过去时间点 → was built。is built 表经常性动作，has built 主动语态，都不对。
--- Q8.6: 改错：The story was happened in a small village during the storm.
  OPTS: [0]was happened → happened | [1]in → at | [2]during → when | [3]small → smaller
  ANSWER(0): was happened → happened
  EXPLAIN: happen 是不及物动词，没有被动语态！was happened 是改错题十大钉子户之一。take place / come true / consist of 同罪，见语法②。
--- Q8.7: 英译汉选择：Every cloud has a silver lining.
  OPTS: [0]每朵云都有一条银色的边。 | [1]黑暗中总有一线光明。 | [2]云的边缘是用银子做成的。 | [3]每片云都会发出银色的光。
  ANSWER(1): 黑暗中总有一线光明。
  EXPLAIN: 谚语不能逐字直译——望文生义是英译汉最大陷阱。本句 = 困境中总有希望，正是钱少的补考剧本，写作收尾万能句。
--- Q8.8: The team's plan consisted ______ three steps: review, practice, and sleep.
  OPTS: [0]of | [1]in | [2]on | [3]for
  ANSWER(0): of
  EXPLAIN: consist of 由……组成，固定介词 of。注意 consist 永远主动——is consisted of 是错的（语法②），同义替换是 be made up of。
--- S8.1: What <mark>happened</mark> in the storm was that a plan was made, notes were shared, and a failure was turned into a starting point.
  PART: What happened in the storm 是主语从句；happen 不及物、无被动，永远不能写 was happened
  PART: 表语从句里三个并列被动：was made / were shared / was turned，be 随主语用 was/were
  PART: turn A into B 的被动式：A was turned into B，A 是动作的承受者
  PART: fail 是动词「挂科」，这件事本身要用名词 failure——词性别混
--- S8.2: Only when you face your failure bravely <mark>can you turn</mark> it into the first step of success.
  PART: Only + 状语从句放句首 → 主句部分倒装：can you turn（改错高频）
  PART: 正常语序：you can turn it into...；倒装只动主句，从句不倒装
  PART: face your failure 直面失败；face 作动词「面对」
  PART: turn A into B 把 A 变成 B

########## CH9 阅读理解 · 古堡谜题 ##########
--- G9.1 t: 定语从句 who / which / that（阅读长难句第一杀手）
  RULE: 先行词（被修饰的名词）是人 → who（作宾语可用 whom，常省略）；是物 → which；多数情况 who/which 都可换成 that。两步判断：先找先行词定人/物，再看从句缺不缺主语或宾语——缺则用关系代词，不缺才轮到关系副词。
  EX: The passage that we read yesterday describes a castle which stands on a lonely hill.
  EXCN: 我们昨天读的那篇短文描写了一座坐落在孤山上的古堡。
  SIGNAL: 标志：名词后紧跟一个完整句子 → 八成是定语从句；坑：that 不能紧跟介词（in which ✓，in that ✗）
--- G9.2 t: 关系副词 where / when / why（从句不缺主宾才用它）
  RULE: 先行词是地点、且从句主谓宾齐全 → where（= in/at which）；先行词是时间 → when；先行词是 reason → why。判断口诀：从句缺主语或宾语 → 用 which/that；从句成分完整、只缺状语 → 用 where/when/why。
  EX: This is the hall where the last king once hid his treasure. I will never forget the day when we entered the castle.
  EXCN: 这就是老国王曾经藏宝的那座大厅。我永远不会忘记我们进古堡的那一天。
  SIGNAL: 坑：the house where I lived ✓（live 不及物，从句缺状语），the house which I visited ✓（visit 及物，从句缺宾语）——选哪个看从句缺什么，不能只看先行词
--- G9.3 t: whose 表所属 与 介词 + whom/which
  RULE: 空格后面紧跟名词、表「谁的」→ 用 whose，人和物都行（the writer whose book... / the house whose roof...）。介词后指人只能用 whom、指物只能用 which，that/who 绝不跟在介词后面。
  EX: The writer whose book won the prize visited the tower, in which a dragon was sleeping.
  EXCN: 他那本书获奖的作家参观了那座塔，塔里正睡着一条龙。
  SIGNAL: 改错高频：which roof ❌ → whose roof ✓（表所属）；介词后 who ❌ → whom ✓；whose 后面必须紧跟名词，这是最快的识别信号
--- Q9.1: What can we ______ from the last paragraph? — The writer's hope for the future.
  OPTS: [0]imply | [1]infer | [2]tell | [3]speak
  ANSWER(1): infer
  EXPLAIN: 读者根据文章推断用 infer。imply 是作者暗示，主语应是 writer，方向反了；tell/speak 不用于此结构。
--- Q9.2: The writer's ______ toward online learning is positive.
  OPTS: [0]attitude | [1]altitude | [2]title | [3]purpose
  ANSWER(0): attitude
  EXPLAIN: attitude toward 对……的态度，态度题核心词。altitude 高度（形近陷阱）、title 标题、purpose 目的。
--- Q9.3: Everyone passed the exam ______ Tom, who missed it because of illness.
  OPTS: [0]besides | [1]except | [2]except for | [3]beside
  ANSWER(1): except
  EXPLAIN: Tom 没参加考试，是从名单里减掉 → except。besides 是加上；beside 在旁边（形近陷阱）；except for 用于整体肯定+局部瑕疵。
--- Q9.4: A dictionary is a book ______ explains the meaning of words.
  OPTS: [0]who | [1]which | [2]whose | [3]where
  ANSWER(1): which
  EXPLAIN: 先行词 book 是物，从句缺主语（______ explains...）→ which/that。who 指人；whose 后必须跟名词；where 表地点且从句要成分完整。
--- Q9.5: This is the ancient hall ______ the last king once hid his treasure.
  OPTS: [0]which | [1]that | [2]where | [3]when
  ANSWER(2): where
  EXPLAIN: 先行词 hall 是地点，从句 hid his treasure 主谓宾齐全、不缺主宾只缺地点状语 → where（= in which）。若从句缺主语或宾语才用 which/that。
--- Q9.6: 改错：The castle which gate is carved with questions has stood here for 500 years.
  OPTS: [0]which → whose | [1]is → are | [2]for → since | [3]years → year
  ANSWER(0): which → whose
  EXPLAIN: gate 前表所属「古堡的大门」必须用 whose（= its gate），which 不能作定语。whose/which 混用是定语从句改错高频雷区。
--- Q9.7: 英译汉选择：He drew on his childhood experience to write the story.
  OPTS: [0]他照着童年的画写出了这个故事。 | [1]他利用童年的经历写出了这个故事。 | [2]他把童年的经历画成了故事。 | [3]他的童年画画得像故事一样。
  ANSWER(1): 他利用童年的经历写出了这个故事。
  EXPLAIN: draw on 固定搭配「利用、动用（经验/知识）」，不能望文生义成「画画」；experience 此处指童年的经历（可数）。英译汉的最大陷阱就是认错熟词的引申义。
--- Q9.8: ______ conclusion, the real treasure of reading is the habit of thinking.
  OPTS: [0]In | [1]On | [2]At | [3]By
  ANSWER(0): In
  EXPLAIN: in conclusion 固定搭配「总而言之」，介词固定 in；= to sum up / in a word，写作结尾万能信号词。
--- S9.1: The writer <mark>mentions</mark> the old woman's experience in the first paragraph in order to show that the new policy has made a real difference to ordinary people's lives.
  PART: mentions...in order to show 提到……是为了说明——写作目的题的标准结构
  PART: in order to + 动词原形表目的，换成 so as to / to 都行
  PART: show that + 宾语从句：说明的内容
  PART: make a difference to 固定搭配「对……产生影响」
--- S9.2: What the writer tries to <mark>suggest</mark> in the last paragraph is that success means nothing unless it is built on what you truly love.
  PART: What the writer tries to suggest 是主语从句：作者想表明的东西
  PART: is that... 表语从句
  PART: unless 引导条件状语从句「除非」，相当于 if...not
  PART: what you truly love 是介词 on 后的宾语从句（用 what 不用 that！）

########## CH10 决战 · 改错考官巨龙 ##########
--- G10.1 t: 宾语从句陈述语序（改错 + 单选双高频）
  RULE: 宾语从句一律用陈述语序：引导词 + 主语 + 谓语。一般疑问句变宾语从句时，先把 if/whether 提到句首，再把 do/does/did 送回主语后面还原成正常语序。I don't know where he lives（✓）≠ where does he live（✗）。
  EX: The dragon asked me where the exit of the cave was, and I told him that I did not know, either.
  EXCN: 巨龙问我洞口在哪里，我告诉它我也不知道。
  SIGNAL: 看到疑问词后面紧跟 do/does/did 或情态动词 → 九成是语序错误；Do you know... / I wonder... / He asked... 后面全踩这个雷
--- G10.2 t: that vs what — 宾语从句引导词二选一
  RULE: 从句本身完整（不缺主语宾语）→ 用 that：只起连接作用，无词义，常可省略。从句缺主语或缺宾语 → 用 what：= the thing(s) that，既连接又充当成分。判断法：把引导词挖掉读一遍，句子完整选 that，缺东西选 what。
  EX: He said that he was tired. / He told me what he needed.
  EXCN: 他说他累了。/ 他告诉了我他需要什么。
  SIGNAL: 改错雷区：said that 后从句完整不能再填 what；I wonder that you can survive ❌ —— survive 前缺成分，用 what 提问用 if/whether 问是非
--- G10.3 t: if/whether 及否定前移（I don't think...）
  RULE: ① 一般疑问句变宾语从句用 if/whether；whether 可与 or not 直接连用（whether or not），if 必须隔开（if...or not ✗）。② think/believe/suppose 接否定从句时，否定要前移到主句：I don't think he is right = 我认为他不对，而不是「我认为他不……」。
  EX: I wonder whether or not the story is true. I don't believe he is a dragon — he is just a tired old man.
  EXCN: 我想知道这个故事是不是真的。我相信他不是龙——他只是一个疲惫的老人。
  SIGNAL: if or not ❌ 只有 whether or not ✓；I think he is not right ❌ → I don't think he is right ✓ 否定前移
--- Q10.1: The teacher gave me some useful ______ on how to learn English.
  OPTS: [0]advices | [1]advice | [2]advise | [3]advising
  ANSWER(1): advice
  EXPLAIN: advice 不可数，没有 advices！advise 是动词。some useful advice 一些有用的建议。
--- Q10.2: I ______ if the old examiner was once a dragon — the story sounds unbelievable.
  OPTS: [0]wander | [1]wonder | [2]attack | [3]agree
  ANSWER(1): wonder
  EXPLAIN: wonder if/whether = 想知道是否；wander 徘徊是形近陷阱；attack 进攻、agree 同意，语义不通。No wonder! 怪不得！
--- Q10.3: ______ many students took part in the sports meeting.
  OPTS: [0]Such | [1]So | [2]Too much | [3]Very
  ANSWER(1): So
  EXPLAIN: 特例：so many/much/few/little + 名词。many 前面用 so 不用 such——改错钉子户。
--- Q10.4: The dragon asked A-Luo ______.
  OPTS: [0]where does the exit go | [1]where the exit goes | [2]where did the exit go | [3]where the exit go
  ANSWER(1): where the exit goes
  EXPLAIN: 宾语从句用陈述语序：where + 主语 the exit + 谓语 goes。A、C 都是疑问语序直接判死；D 缺三单 s。
--- Q10.5: Can you tell me ______ the word furniture means?
  OPTS: [0]that | [1]what | [2]which | [3]if
  ANSWER(1): what
  EXPLAIN: means 缺宾语 → 用 what（既连接又充当成分）。that 只连接不成分，用于完整从句；if 表是否，语义不通。
--- Q10.6: 改错：He asked me that I had finished the task on time.
  OPTS: [0]that → whether | [1]me → I | [2]finished → finish | [3]on → in
  ANSWER(0): that → whether
  EXPLAIN: 原句是一般疑问句变宾语从句，要用 if/whether 引导，that 用于陈述句变从句。asked sb. whether... 是否类宾语从句的改错高频点。
--- Q10.7: 英译汉选择：You can't be too careful when you correct your composition.
  OPTS: [0]你修改作文时不能太认真。 | [1]修改作文时再怎么仔细都不为过。 | [2]你不能太认真地修改作文吗？ | [3]改作文太仔细是行不通的。
  ANSWER(1): 修改作文时再怎么仔细都不为过。
  EXPLAIN: can't...too... 固定句式 = 再……也不为过。望文生义翻译成「不能太认真」就掉坑了——真题翻译最爱考这类否定陷阱。
--- Q10.8: Ten years later, the old examiner finally agreed ______ the students' plan and agreed ______ them that mistakes could be corrected.
  OPTS: [0]to; with | [1]with; to | [2]with; with | [3]to; to
  ANSWER(0): to; with

########## CH11 时光博物馆 · 会说话的文物 ##########
--- G11.1 t: 主语从句 — What 放句首（从句缺啥用啥）
  RULE: What 引导主语从句放在句首，意为「……的东西/事情」，what 在从句中作主语或宾语；整个从句作主语时，谓语动词用单数。对比：that 引导主语从句时从句必须结构完整（that 无词义、不作成分）；whether 引导时表「是否」。口诀：从句缺主语或宾语 → what；从句什么都不缺 → that。
  EX: What the bronze cup said about the Tang Dynasty surprised every visitor.
  EXCN: 青铜爵讲的关于唐朝的事让每位游客大吃一惊。
  SIGNAL: 空在句首且从句内缺成分 → 秒选 what；改错雷区②：What 从句作主语谓语用单数——What he said were ❌ → was
--- G11.2 t: 同位语从句 — belief/doubt/idea/news + that
  RULE: 抽象名词 news / idea / belief / doubt / fact / hope / truth 后接 that 从句，说明这个名词的具体内容，即同位语从句。特征：that 只起连接作用，不在从句中作任何成分，但不可省略，也不能换成 which。与定语从句一秒区分：定语从句的 that 在从句里作主语或宾语；同位语从句的从句结构完整、不缺成分。
  EX: The belief that these treasures can speak is not crazy — just come at midnight.
  EXCN: 「这些珍宝会说话」的信念并不疯狂——午夜来一趟就知道了。
  SIGNAL: news/idea/belief/doubt + that + 完整句 → 同位语从句；真题爱把 that 换成 what 挖坑：The news what... ❌
--- G11.3 t: It is believed that / It is said that（据说家族）
  RULE: It is said that... 据说 / It is believed that... 人们认为 / It is reported that... 据报道 / It is well-known that... 众所周知。it 是形式主语，真正的主语是后面的 that 从句（和 ch01 的 It takes sb...to do 是一家人）。同义转换：It is said that he is a good guide. = He is said to be a good guide.
  EX: It is said that the museum comes alive at midnight, and it is believed that every artifact remembers its own dynasty.
  EXCN: 据说这家博物馆午夜会活过来，而且人们相信每件文物都记得自己的朝代。
  SIGNAL: It is said/believed/reported 后必须接完整 that 从句；改错坑：It says that ❌（必须用被动 is said）

  EXPLAIN: 文化遗产 = cultural heritage，固定搭配且不可数。habit 是习惯（ch02 复现老友），holiday 假日，harvest 收获，三个都是形近干扰。

  OPTS: [0]that | [1]which | [2]what | [3]whether
  ANSWER(0): that

  EXPLAIN: 改错雷区②主谓一致：What 引导的主语从句作主语，谓语用第三人称单数 → was。said 的过去时由 at midnight 语境锁定，正确；beyond one's imagination 超出想象，固定搭配无误。
--- Q11.8: 英译汉选择：The old professor is a walking dictionary of Chinese history.
  OPTS: [0]老教授是一本会走路的词典，搬起来特别沉。 | [1]老教授对中国历史了如指掌，堪称活字典。 | [2]老教授散步时总爱读历史词典。 | [3]老教授把词典一路走一路送人。

  PART: There is no doubt that... 同位语从句：that 从句说明 doubt 的具体内容

  PART: a piece of 一件：heritage 不可数，计数必须借单位（呼应 ch10 的 a piece of advice 套路）

--- S11.2: What makes the museum special is that every <mark>treasure</mark> behind the glass carries a story worth listening to.
  PART: What makes the museum special 主语从句：让博物馆特别的东西
  PART: is that... 表语从句
  PART: carry a story 承载故事；behind the glass 介词短语作后置定语
  PART: worth listening to：be worth doing 主动表被动（复现 ch07）；listen to 的 to 不能丢

########## CH12 飞驰大陆 · 迟到的列车 ##########
--- G12.1 t: 现在分词 doing 作状语（主动/进行）
  RULE: doing 作状语时，逻辑主语 = 主句主语，且是主动关系。放句首多表时间/原因（Hearing the news, ... 一听到消息），放句尾表伴随（..., shouting and pushing 又喊又挤）。⚠️ 悬垂分词陷阱：如果主句主语做不了这个动作，句子就是错的。
  EX: Hearing the announcement, the passengers rushed to the platform, shouting and pushing.
  EXCN: 一听到广播，乘客们就冲向站台，又喊又挤。
  SIGNAL: 标志：逗号 + doing。判断口诀：主语能主动做 → doing；只能被动承受 → done。改错爱在 Seeing/Seen 上挖坑
--- G12.2 t: 过去分词 done 作定语（被动/完成）
  RULE: done 与被修饰名词是被动或完成关系：单个 done 放名词前（a delayed train 晚点的火车），done 短语放名词后（the road built last year 去年修的路）= the road (that was) built last year。⚠️ 别和 doing 定语混：a boring trip 主动「令人无聊」/ a bored passenger 被动「感到无聊」。
  EX: The road built last year has greatly reduced the traffic jams in the city.
  EXCN: 去年修建的那条路大大减少了城里的堵车。
  SIGNAL: done = 「被……的」；seeing → seen 一字之差，主动被动天壤之别；改错雷区⑦非谓语高频
--- G12.3 t: have difficulty/trouble (in) doing
  RULE: have difficulty (in) doing sth. = have trouble (in) doing sth. 做某事有困难。in 可以省略，但后面必须接 doing，绝不能改成 to do。同族句型：be busy (in) doing / spend time (in) doing。
  EX: They had great difficulty getting out of the traffic jam, so they missed the first train.
  EXCN: 他们很难从堵车中脱身，所以错过了第一班火车。
  SIGNAL: 看到 difficulty / trouble / busy → 后面动词用 doing！have difficulty to do ❌ 是改错钉子户
--- Q12.1: The heavy snow had a bad ______ on the traffic.
  OPTS: [0]affect | [1]effect | [2]effort | [3]afford
  ANSWER(1): effect
  EXPLAIN: 复现 ch01：空前 had a bad，缺名词 → effect。affect 是动词；effort 努力；afford 买得起。
--- Q12.2: The flight was ______ for three hours because of the storm.
  OPTS: [0]delayed | [1]arrived | [2]departed | [3]landed
  ANSWER(0): delayed
  EXPLAIN: 因为暴风雨，航班「被延误」→ 被动语态 was delayed。arrived/departed/landed 与 because of the storm 逻辑不符。
--- Q12.3: The price of train tickets keeps ______ every year.
  OPTS: [0]raising | [1]rising | [2]raised | [3]risen
  ANSWER(1): rising
  EXPLAIN: 复现 ch06：价格自己上涨 → rise 不及物；keep doing 后接 doing → rising。raise 是及物动词要接宾语。
--- Q12.4: ______ the red light, the driver stopped the car at once.
  OPTS: [0]Seeing | [1]Seen | [2]Saw | [3]To seeing
  ANSWER(0): Seeing
  EXPLAIN: 语法①：司机「主动看见」红灯 → 现在分词 Seeing 作状语，逻辑主语 = the driver。Seen 表被动 ❌；Saw 是谓语动词，逗号后不能再放一个谓语。

  OPTS: [0]to find | [1]finding | [2]find | [3]found
  ANSWER(1): finding
  EXPLAIN: 语法③：have difficulty (in) doing 固定句型，in 可省略，动词必须用 doing。have difficulty to find ❌。
--- Q12.6: 改错：The bridge build in 1990 is still in use now.
  OPTS: [0]build → built | [1]in → on | [2]is → was | [3]use → using
  ANSWER(0): build → built
  EXPLAIN: 语法②：桥是「被修建」的 → 过去分词 built 作后置定语 = the bridge that was built in 1990。改错雷区⑦非谓语：done 表被动。
--- Q12.7: 英译汉选择：He got up at five to catch the first train.
  OPTS: [0]他五点起床去抓住火车的第一节车厢。 | [1]他五点起床去赶第一班火车。 | [2]他五点起床去追赶第一辆火车。 | [3]他五点起床时被火车抓住了。

  EXPLAIN: catch + 交通工具 = 赶上车，不是「抓住」。望文生义是英译汉的最大陷阱；同类：miss the train 是错过火车，不是想念火车。

  EXPLAIN: be crowded with 固定搭配「挤满」。⚠️ be full of 才用 of，两个介词别混。

  PART: which covers... 非限制性定语从句修饰 railway，逗号不能少

########## CH13 记忆宫殿 · 遗忘之雾 ##########
--- G13.1 t: 过去完成时 had done —— 过去的过去
  RULE: 两个过去的动作，先发生的用 had done，后发生的用一般过去时。三大标志：① by the time + 过去时从句（到……时候为止）② before / after 连接两个过去动作 ③ by + 过去时间点（by last year / by ten o'clock）。
  EX: By the time the beast reached the hall, A-Luo had reviewed the whole list twice.
  EXCN: 等怪兽走到大厅时，阿洛已经把词表复习了两遍。
  SIGNAL: 看到 by the time + 过去式从句，主句秒选 had done；改错雷区：By the time he came, I already finished ❌ → had already finished
--- G13.2 t: hardly...when / no sooner...than —— 刚……就……
  RULE: 固定配对不能混搭：hardly 配 when，no sooner 配 than。陈述语序：I had hardly sat down when the phone rang. 倒装考点：Hardly had I sat down when...（hardly / no sooner 放句首，had 提到主语前）。
  EX: Hardly had A-Luo recalled the first word when the beast ate another light.
  EXCN: 阿洛刚回忆起第一个词，怪兽就又吃掉了一盏灯。
  SIGNAL: 三连坑：① hardly 配 when、no sooner 配 than，改错最爱交叉混搭 ② 句首倒装必须 had 提前 ③ when / than 从句用一般过去时，不用完成时
--- G13.3 t: 过去进行时 was/were doing + when —— 正在……这时突然……
  RULE: 过去某一时刻正在进行 → was/were doing。经典句型：was doing...when + 一般过去时，长动作被短动作打断。when 表「这时突然」，此处不能换 while；while 后接进行时表「与此同时」。
  EX: A-Luo was drawing red circles around the words he kept forgetting when the beast broke in.
  EXCN: 阿洛正在给总忘的词画红圈，这时怪兽闯了进来。
  SIGNAL: 改错雷区：was doing...when 的 when 从句不能再用进行时；when 前后两个动作一长一短 → 长 doing 短 did
--- Q13.1: A good ______ is better than hard work. Find your own way to remember words.
  OPTS: [0]method | [1]memory | [2]mistake | [3]medal
  ANSWER(0): method
  EXPLAIN: method 方法——找对方法胜过死努力，本章主题词。memory 记忆，mistake 错误，medal 奖牌。
--- Q13.2: You will forget what you learned today unless you ______ it in time.
  OPTS: [0]review | [1]remind | [2]recall | [3]reply
  ANSWER(0): review
  EXPLAIN: 复现 ch01：review 复习 = 及时再看，遗忘曲线的头号克星。remind 是提醒别人，recall 是自己回忆，reply 是回复。
--- Q13.3: This old palace ______ me of the small town where I grew up.
  OPTS: [0]reminds | [1]recalls | [2]remembers | [3]forgets
  ANSWER(0): reminds
  EXPLAIN: remind sb. of sth. 使某人想起，主语是外物（宫殿）。recall / remember 不接 of 结构。
--- Q13.4: By the time the fog reached the fourth room, A-Luo ______ the whole word list twice.
  OPTS: [0]reviewed | [1]had reviewed | [2]has reviewed | [3]was reviewing
  ANSWER(1): had reviewed
  EXPLAIN: by the time + 过去时从句（reached）→ 主句用过去完成时 had done，表示「过去的过去」。reviewed 是一般过去时，has reviewed 是现在完成时，was reviewing 是过去进行时。
--- Q13.5: He ______ the words aloud when the light suddenly went out.
  OPTS: [0]read | [1]was reading | [2]has read | [3]had read
  ANSWER(1): was reading
  EXPLAIN: was/were doing...when「正在做……这时突然」——长动作用过去进行时，短动作 went out 用一般过去时打断它。
--- Q13.6: Concentrate ______ one room at a time, or the fog will eat you alive.
  OPTS: [0]at | [1]on | [2]in | [3]for
  ANSWER(1): on
  EXPLAIN: 复现 ch01：concentrate on 固定搭配，介词只能用 on；= focus on。
--- Q13.7: 改错：Hardly had he entered the palace than the lights went out.
  OPTS: [0]than → when | [1]had → has | [2]entered → enter | [3]went → gone
  ANSWER(0): than → when
  EXPLAIN: hardly...when / no sooner...than 是固定配对，不能交叉混搭：hardly 必须配 when（或者把 hardly 换成 no sooner）。本章语法②经典改错雷区。
--- Q13.8: 英译汉选择：I'm sorry, but your name has escaped me.
  OPTS: [0]对不起，你的名字从我身边逃跑了。 | [1]对不起，我一时想不起你的名字了。 | [2]对不起，你的名字把我甩掉了。 | [3]对不起，我躲避了你的名字。

  EXPLAIN: escape 望文生义陷阱：sth. escapes sb. = 某人想不起某事，是「忘记」的委婉说法，和逃跑没有关系。
--- S13.1: He had hardly <mark>recalled</mark> the first ten words when the beast ate another light, and by the time Juanjuan <mark>explained</mark> the <mark>method</mark> again, the fog had swallowed half of the palace.
  PART: had hardly done...when...：hardly...when「刚……就……」，主句过去完成时，when 从句用一般过去时

  PART: keep a note of = take notes of 记下、记下来

########## CH14 夜市大冒险 · 五百元的晚餐 ##########
--- G14.1 t: 感官系动词 look / sound / smell / taste / feel + adj.
  RULE: 这五个词是「感受翻译官」：后面接形容词，不接副词——The soup tastes good（不是 well）。它们是系动词，主动形式表被动感觉，没有进行时也不用被动（is tasted ❌）。后接名词要加 like：It sounds like a good idea。feel 还可接 tired / relaxed 这类分词形容词。
  EX: The night market smells wonderful, the grilled fish tastes delicious, and the music sounds a bit loud.
  EXCN: 夜市闻起来很香，烤鱼尝起来很美味，音乐听起来有点吵。
  SIGNAL: 改错双雷区：① smell well ❌ → smell good（系动词后用形容词）② is tasted ❌ → tastes（无被动）。看到这五个词，后面找 adj 而不是 adv
--- G14.2 t: get sth. done / have sth. done —— 请人做；遭遇
  RULE: have/get + 宾语 + 过去分词：① 请人做某事：have my hair cut 理发（头发是别人剪的）② 遭遇不幸：had his wallet stolen 钱包被偷。done 表被动，宾语是「被打理/被遭遇」的对象。三兄弟对比：have sb. do 让某人做（原形）、have sb. doing 让某人一直做、have sth. done 某事被做。
  EX: Qian-Shao had his photo taken with ten stall owners, and got his phone screen fixed at the market.
  EXCN: 钱少和十个摊主合了影，还在市场上把手机屏幕修好了。
  SIGNAL: 改错雷区：have sth. do ❌ → have sth. done（宾语是被动方，用过去分词）；经典陷阱 cut：have my hair cut 里 cut 是过去分词，不是原形
--- G14.3 t: would rather do (than do) —— 宁愿……也不……
  RULE: would rather + 动词原形：I would rather stay。加比较：would rather do A than do B，than 前后对称、都用原形，不能加 to。否定：would rather not do。缩写：'d rather。若后接从句则用虚拟语气（第 18 章再会）。
  EX: I would rather drink bitter melon juice than touch junk food again. Pang-Da said he would rather not eat before the sports meet.
  EXCN: 我宁愿喝苦瓜汁也不想再碰垃圾食品。胖达说他运动会前宁愿不吃东西。
  SIGNAL: 改错头号雷区：would rather to do ❌ → would rather do；than 后面同样用原形：would rather stay than to go ❌ → than go
--- Q14.1: In a Western dinner, ______ is usually served at the end of the meal.
  OPTS: [0]dessert | [1]desert | [2]snack | [3]recipe
  ANSWER(0): dessert
  EXPLAIN: 甜点饭后上 → dessert（双 s，想吃两次所以两个 s）。desert 一个 s 是沙漠！snack 是正餐之间的零食，recipe 是配方。
--- Q14.2: Following grandma's secret ______, the cook made the fish both sweet and spicy.
  OPTS: [0]recipe | [1]receipt | [2]menu | [3]order
  ANSWER(0): recipe
  EXPLAIN: recipe 配方/食谱，三音节 /ˈresəpi/；receipt 是收据（/rɪˈsiːt/，p 不发音），长得像意思远；menu 菜单；order 点菜。
--- Q14.3: My uncle is a good ______ and works in a big hotel.
  OPTS: [0]cook | [1]cooker | [2]cooking | [3]cooked
  ANSWER(0): cook
  EXPLAIN: 厨师 = cook（人）；cooker 是炊具（锅）不是人！说 a good cooker 等于说人是口锅——胡爷爷亲自吐槽过的世纪冤案。
--- Q14.4: The soup ______ a bit salty. Could you bring me another one?
  OPTS: [0]is tasted | [1]tastes | [2]taste | [3]tasting
  ANSWER(1): tastes
  EXPLAIN: 本章语法①：taste 感官系动词「尝起来」，主动形式无被动（is tasted ❌）；后面接形容词 salty；soup 单数 → tastes。
--- Q14.5: Qian-Shao ______ his wallet ______ on the crowded night market last week.
  OPTS: [0]had; steal | [1]had; stolen | [2]has; stolen | [3]had; stealing
  ANSWER(1): had; stolen
  EXPLAIN: 本章语法②：have sth. done 遭遇某事，钱包是「被偷」→ 过去分词 stolen（steal-stole-stolen）；had sth. do ❌。
--- Q14.6: 改错：I would rather to stay at home and cook than eat that dark cuisine again.
  OPTS: [0]to stay → stay | [1]and → or | [2]eat → ate | [3]than → then
  ANSWER(0): to stay → stay
  EXPLAIN: 本章语法③：would rather 后接动词原形，不加 to；than 前后对称都用原形（stay...eat）。
--- Q14.7: 英译汉选择：The noodles at that stall are second to none.
  OPTS: [0]那个摊位的面条排第二，没有第一。 | [1]那个摊位的面条首屈一指。 | [2]那个摊位没有面条可排。 | [3]那个摊位的面条没人排第二次。
  ANSWER(1): 那个摊位的面条首屈一指。
  EXPLAIN: second to none 固定习语「首屈一指、无人能敌」，不是「排第二」。英译汉最大的坑就是望文生义。
--- Q14.8: This stall is famous ______ its grilled fish — people wait an hour for it.
  OPTS: [0]for | [1]as | [2]of | [3]with
  ANSWER(0): for
  EXPLAIN: 复现 ch11：因事物闻名 = be famous for；因身份闻名 = be famous as（Grandpa Hu is famous as a cook）。
--- S14.1: Although junk food <mark>tastes</mark> delicious, it contains too much sugar and fat, which does great harm to our health.
  PART: ⚠️ although 引导让步从句，主句前绝不能加 but（复现 ch10：中文「虽然…但是」直译陷阱）
  PART: tastes 感官系动词 + 形容词 delicious「尝起来美味」，主动形式无被动（本章语法①主角）
  PART: too much + 不可数名词（sugar / fat）；too many + 可数复数
  PART: which 引导非限制性定语从句，指代前面整件事；do harm to 对……有害
--- S14.2: The old cook <mark>had</mark> his secret menu <mark>rewritten</mark> so that the dish would taste good and keep people full of <mark>energy</mark>.
  PART: had sth. done 让别人做：菜单是「被重写」的 → rewrite-rewrote-rewritten 用过去分词（本章语法②核心）
  PART: so that 引导目的状语从句：为了既好吃又顶饱
  PART: keep + 宾语 + adj.：keep people full 保持饱腹；be full of energy 精力充沛（复现 ch05）
  PART: would taste 过去将来时配合主句 had rewritten，时态前后呼应一致

########## CH15 运动会风云 · 一秒的差距 ##########
--- G15.1 t: 过去进行时 vs 一般过去（while 两长一短）
  RULE: 过去进行时 was/were doing：过去某时「正在进行」的动作，作背景，偏长；一般过去时 did：过去发生并完成的短动作、新事件。while 表一段时间，从句常配进行时；when + 短动作常「打断」进行中的长动作。口诀：when 短 while 长，两长可同框。
  EX: While the coach was explaining the plan, Pang-Da fell asleep, and the audience were cheering loudly at that time.
  EXCN: 教练正在讲解战术时，胖达睡着了；那时观众正在高声欢呼。
  SIGNAL: 标志词：while / at that time / at eight last night / this time yesterday。改错坑：把 While...was doing 改成 did，或把 when 后的短动作硬改成进行时
--- G15.2 t: play / go / do 运动搭配三选一
  RULE: play + 球类棋类名词：play football / basketball / chess（球类前不加 the，乐器才加：play the piano）；go + 运动-ing：go swimming / skating / boating / fishing（出行动感型）；do + 锻炼类名词：do sports / exercise / kung fu / yoga。
  EX: He plays football on Monday, goes swimming in summer, and does sports to keep fit.
  EXCN: 他周一踢足球，夏天去游泳，平时做运动保持身材。
  SIGNAL: 改错高频：play swimming ❌ → go swimming；play the basketball ❌ → play basketball。口诀：球棋 play，水陆 go，锻炼 do
--- G15.3 t: 不定式作目的状语：in order to / so as to
  RULE: 「为了做某事」：in order to do（句首句中都行）、so as to do（只能句中，不能放句首）、直接 to do（最口语）。否定目的：in order not to do / so as not to do。后接完整从句时改用 in order that / so that + 从句（常配 can/could）。
  EX: In order to catch the early bus, he got up at five. He got up early so as not to miss it.
  EXCN: 为了赶上早班车，他五点就起床了。他早起是为了不错过车。
  SIGNAL: 改错双坑：①So as to 放句首 ❌ ②in order to seeing ❌ → to 是不定式符号，后面必须接动词原形
--- Q15.1: The match attracted a large ______ of over 20,000 people.
  OPTS: [0]audience | [1]audiences | [2]viewer | [3]watcher
  ANSWER(0): audience
  EXPLAIN: audience 观众（集合名词，不加 s）：a large audience 一大群观众。viewer 是（电视）观看者，watcher 是监视者。
--- Q15.2: They suffered a heavy ______ in the first game, but they didn't lose heart.
  OPTS: [0]victory | [1]defeat | [2]win | [3]prize
  ANSWER(1): defeat
  EXPLAIN: suffer a defeat 遭受失败固定搭配；后面说「没灰心」说明先输了。victory/win 是胜利，与语境相反；prize 是奖品。
--- Q15.3: Our team ______ theirs 3:2 in the final match.
  OPTS: [0]won | [1]beat | [2]got | [3]hit
  ANSWER(1): beat
  EXPLAIN: beat + 对手（人/队）→ beat theirs。win 后接「物」：won the match/the game。won theirs ❌，见本章易混组。
--- Q15.4: While Muscle Bro ______ the players how to pass the ball, Pang-Da fell asleep on the grass.
  OPTS: [0]was showing | [1]showed | [2]shows | [3]is showing
  ANSWER(0): was showing
  EXPLAIN: while 引导的从句表「正在进行」的背景动作 → 过去进行时 was showing；后面 fell asleep 是一般过去时短动作插入——when 短 while 长。
--- Q15.5: In winter, children like to ______ skating on the frozen lake.
  OPTS: [0]do | [1]play | [2]go | [3]make
  ANSWER(2): go
  EXPLAIN: go + 运动-ing：go skating 去滑冰。play 接球类名词，do 接 sports/exercise。口诀：球棋 play，水陆 go，锻炼 do。
--- Q15.6: 改错：In order to winning the relay race, the team trained every morning.
  OPTS: [0]winning → win | [1]trained → training | [2]race → racing | [3]in → on
  ANSWER(0): winning → win
  EXPLAIN: 非谓语雷区：in order to 后接动词原形表目的 → in order to win。这里 to 是不定式符号不是介词，后面不能接 doing。
--- Q15.7: 英译汉选择：To everyone's surprise, the dark horse beat the defending champion in the final.
  OPTS: [0]让所有人惊讶的是，那匹黑色的马在决赛中打败了卫冕冠军。 | [1]让所有人惊讶的是，那位爆冷的黑马选手在决赛中击败了卫冕冠军。 | [2]让所有人惊讶的是，黑马选手在决赛中被卫冕冠军击败了。 | [3]大家惊喜地发现，黑马和卫冕冠军在决赛中打成了平手。
  ANSWER(1): 让所有人惊讶的是，那位爆冷的黑马选手在决赛中击败了卫冕冠军。
  EXPLAIN: dark horse 黑马 = 实力隐藏、爆冷取胜的人，不是真的马；beat the champion 主动击败（beat + 人）。望文生义是英译汉选择的第一陷阱。
--- Q15.8: Run faster, A-Luo! At this speed you will never catch ______ with the runner ahead of you.
  OPTS: [0]up | [1]on | [2]over | [3]back
  ANSWER(0): up
  EXPLAIN: catch up with 固定搭配「追上，赶上」；反义 fall behind 落后。题干里的 at this speed 和 ahead of 也都是本章考点词。
--- S15.1: No matter how difficult the <mark>match</mark> is, the players never <mark>lose heart</mark>, because they believe that team spirit matters more than the final <mark>score</mark>.
  PART: No matter how + adj. 「无论多么……」让步状语从句（= however difficult）
  PART: ⚠️ lose heart 灰心 ≠ lose one's heart 爱上——丢了 the 心是坠入爱河，丢了 heart 是泄气
  PART: matter 是动词「要紧」：team spirit matters 团队精神要紧
  PART: more...than... 比较结构：A matters more than B
--- S15.2: It was the first time that our school team had <mark>defeated</mark> the defending <mark>champion</mark>, which greatly encouraged all the students on campus.
  PART: It was the first time that + 过去完成时：固定句型「这是第一次……」（was → had done）
  PART: had defeated 过去完成时：击败发生在「这是第一次」之前，过去的过去
  PART: which 引导非限制性定语从句，指代前面整件事「校队击败卫冕冠军」
  PART: encourage v. 鼓舞（复现 ch08）；on campus 在校园里（复现 ch01）

########## CH16 山水旅行 · 假期副本 ##########
--- G16.1 t: 将来时家族：will / be going to / be about to / plan to
  RULE: 四兄弟都表将来，分工不同：① will do 临场决定、承诺、预测（I will help you.）② be going to do 事先计划好，或有迹象表明马上要发生（Look at the clouds — it is going to rain.）③ be about to do 刚要、即将（后面不接 tomorrow 等具体时间点）④ plan to do 打算做，重在计划。
  EX: We plan to watch the sunset by the lake. Look at the black clouds — it is going to rain. Don't worry, I will call the hotel right now.
  EXCN: 我们打算在湖边看日落。看那些乌云——快要下雨了。别担心，我马上给旅馆打电话。
  SIGNAL: 事先安排 → be going to / plan to；说话时才决定、承诺 → will；眼看就要发生 → be about to / be going to；改错坑：be about to leave tomorrow ❌（about to 后不加具体时间状语）
--- G16.2 t: 主将从现：时间 / 条件从句里的将来
  RULE: 主句用将来时（will do），if / when / as soon as / before / after / until 引导的时间或条件从句必须用一般现在时表将来。口诀：主将从现——将军（will）待在主句，士兵（现在时）蹲守从句。
  EX: If it rains tomorrow, we will stay at the hotel and play cards. As soon as we arrive at the lake, I will take photos of the sunset.
  EXCN: 如果明天下雨，我们就待在旅馆里打牌。我们一到湖边，就给日落拍照。
  SIGNAL: 单选看到 if / when / as soon as + 空格，主句里有 will → 从句选现在时；改错坑：If it will rain ❌ → rains；⚠️ if 表「是否」引导宾语从句时不受此限：I don't know if it will rain.
--- G16.3 t: 现在完成时复习：for 段 since 点
  RULE: have/has + 过去分词，过去发生并持续到现在。for + 时间段（for five years），since + 过去时间点（since 2019 / since he graduated）。口诀：for 接一段，since 接一点。能延续的动词（live / work / study / wait）才能和时间段连用。
  EX: He has worked as a tour guide since he graduated from college, and he has lived by the lake for ten years.
  EXCN: 他大学毕业后就一直当导游，已经在湖边住了十年。
  SIGNAL: since + 点（2019 / last year / 一般过去时从句），for + 段（ten years / a long time）；改错坑：has come here for five years ❌（come 非延续动词）→ has been here for five years
--- Q16.1: The ______ from the hotel window is wonderful — you can see the whole lake.
  OPTS: [0]view | [1]scenery | [2]scene | [3]sight
  ANSWER(0): view
  EXPLAIN: 从某处「看到的」景色 = view。scenery 是大范围自然风光总称（不可数）；scene 是一幕场景/现场。
--- Q16.2: At the airport, he found two ______ were missing.
  OPTS: [0]luggages | [1]pieces of luggage | [2]luggage | [3]bag of luggage
  ANSWER(1): pieces of luggage
  EXPLAIN: ⚠️ luggage 不可数，没有 luggages！两件行李 = two pieces of luggage（复数落在 piece 上，所以谓语用 were）。
--- Q16.3: Life is a long ______; enjoy every step of it.
  OPTS: [0]trip | [1]travel | [2]tour | [3]journey
  ANSWER(3): journey
  EXPLAIN: 复现 ch12：人生长途 → journey（长途旅程）。trip 短途一趟；tour 观光线路；travel 是总概念不可数。
--- Q16.4: Look at the black clouds! It ______ rain.
  OPTS: [0]will | [1]is going to | [2]was going to | [3]plans
  ANSWER(1): is going to
  EXPLAIN: 有迹象（乌云）表明马上要发生 → be going to。will 多表临场决定或承诺；plans to 后面必须接动词原形；was going to 是过去将来时。
--- Q16.5: My uncle has worked as a tour guide ______ he graduated from college.
  OPTS: [0]for | [1]since | [2]from | [3]after
  ANSWER(1): since
  EXPLAIN: since + 过去时间点/一般过去时从句（he graduated），for + 时间段。现在完成时 for 段 since 点，河南真题单选高频。
--- Q16.6: 改错：If it will rain tomorrow, we will stay at the hotel and play cards.
  OPTS: [0]will rain → rains | [1]will stay → stay | [2]and → or | [3]at → in
  ANSWER(0): will rain → rains
  EXPLAIN: 主将从现雷区：if 条件从句用一般现在时表将来 → rains；主句的 will stay 才用将来时。and 表并列正确，不用改 or。
--- Q16.7: 英译汉选择：The sunset by the lake took our breath away.
  OPTS: [0]湖上的日落把我们的呼吸带走了。 | [1]湖上的日落美得让我们屏住了呼吸。 | [2]湖上的日落偷走了我们的空气。 | [3]湖上的日落让我们上气不接下气。
  ANSWER(1): 湖上的日落美得让我们屏住了呼吸。
  EXPLAIN: take one's breath away 固定搭配「美得令人窒息」，不是字面的「拿走呼吸」。望文生义是英译汉选择的第一大陷阱。
--- Q16.8: We arrived ______ the small hotel near the lake just before dark.
  OPTS: [0]at | [1]to | [2]in | [3]for
  ANSWER(0): at
  EXPLAIN: arrive at + 小地点（hotel / station / village），arrive in + 大地方（city / country）；旅馆是具体小地点 → at。reach 后面则直接加地点不加介词。
--- S16.1: If you <mark>book</mark> the wrong <mark>hotel</mark> in a strange city, don't cry, because the wrong road often leads to the right <mark>scenery</mark>.
  PART: if 条件状语从句用一般现在时 book 表将来，主句用祈使句 don't cry——「主将从现」的祈使句版（本章语法②）
  PART: in a strange city 介词短语作地点状语；strange 陌生的，修饰 city
  PART: because 引导原因状语从句：the wrong road often leads to the right scenery
  PART: lead to 中的 to 是介词，后接名词；scenery 风景总称不可数，前面不加 a
--- S16.2: This is the most unforgettable <mark>experience</mark> that we have shared since we came to Qingmeng Academy, and its sweet <mark>memory</mark> will stay with us for years.
  PART: the most unforgettable 多音节形容词最高级：the most + adj.
  PART: that we have shared 定语从句修饰 experience（这里取「经历」义，可数）
  PART: have shared 现在完成时 + since 引导的过去时间点从句——for 接时间段、since 接时间点（本章语法③）
  PART: and 并列第二个主句：will stay 一般将来时，for years 时间段与前面的 since 点形成对照

########## CH17 网络时代 · 热搜风暴 ##########
--- G17.1 t: 宾语从句的时态呼应（主过从过 + 真理例外）
  RULE: 主句是过去时（said/told/asked/knew）→ 从句必须用过去范畴的时态：一般过去时、过去进行时、过去完成时、过去将来时（would do）。唯一例外：从句讲的是客观真理或自然规律 → 永远用一般现在时。
  EX: He said that the Tower was safe, and added that light travels faster than sound.
  EXCN: 他说塔是安全的，还补充说光比声音传播得快。
  SIGNAL: 主句过去时 + 从句 is/will/has done = 改错雷区①时态不一致；但真理句（The teacher said that the earth goes around the sun）别手痒改成 went
--- G17.2 t: 直接引语 → 间接引语（时态前移 + 人称变化）
  RULE: 引号消失后做四件事：①时态前移一格：am/is→was，are→were，will→would，can→could，have done→had done；②人称随主语变：I→he/she；③时间地点跟着走：now→then，today→that day，here→there，this→that；④陈述句用 that 引导，一般疑问句用 if/whether 且改成陈述语序（复现 ch10）。
  EX: She said, 「I will post the report here today.」 → She said that she would post the report there that day.
  EXCN: 她说她那天会在那里发报告。
  SIGNAL: 改错最爱挖人称：He said that I was tired ❌（别人转述你，得说 he was）；疑问句变间接后必须陈述语序，出现 will ❌
--- G17.3 t: would like sb to do / look forward to doing

########## CH18 灯塔老人 · 独行但不孤独 ##########
--- G18.1 t: wish + 过去式虚拟：愿望落空专用（单选高频）
  RULE: wish 后的宾语从句必须用虚拟语气：与现在事实相反 → 过去式（be 一律用 were）；与过去事实相反 → had done。核心逻辑：真正的愿望，都是现实里还没有的东西，所以时态要「后退一步」。
  EX: I wish I were a young sailor again. He wishes he had kept the light earlier.
  EXCN: 我真希望自己还是个年轻水手。他真希望自己早点守上这盏灯。
  SIGNAL: 看到 wish 别再填 will/can：现在的事用过去式，过去的事用 had done。注意区分 hope（可能实现的希望，接陈述语气）
--- G18.2 t: It is high time that + did：该是……的时候了（写作模板句）
  RULE: It is (high/about) time that sb. did sth. = 该是做某事的时候了（其实早就该做了）。that 从句用过去式，或 should + 动词原形（should 不可省）。另一句型 It is time for sb. to do sth. 用的是 to do，别混。
  EX: It is high time that we made up our minds about the future.
  EXCN: 该是我们对未来下定决心的时候了。
  SIGNAL: 从句动词用过去式是铁律：It is high time that we make ❌ → made ✓；写作里用它收尾，阅卷老师眼前一亮
--- G18.3 t: as if / as though 好像：虚拟与真实的分界线
  RULE: as if / as though（好像）引导从句：内容与事实相反时用虚拟——与现在相反 → did/were；与过去相反 → had done。若从句内容可能是真的，用陈述语气。
  EX: The old man talks about the sea as if he had sailed it for a hundred years. He looks as if he is tired.
  EXCN: 老人谈起大海，好像已经航行了一百年（其实没有——虚拟）。他看起来好像累了（可能真累——陈述）。
  SIGNAL: 判断真假再选时态：明知不可能（人活不了一百岁）→ had done；可能是事实 → 正常时态。单选看到 as if 先问：这是比喻还是事实？
--- Q18.1: He lives ______ on a small island, but he never feels ______.
  OPTS: [0]alone; lonely | [1]lonely; alone | [2]alone; alone | [3]lonely; lonely
  ANSWER(0): alone; lonely
  EXPLAIN: 复现 ch08：alone 客观独处（独自住），lonely 内心孤独（感到孤独）。经典搭配 live alone / feel lonely——独处不等于孤独。
--- Q18.2: After ten years of hard work, he finally ______ in rebuilding the lighthouse.
  OPTS: [0]succeeded | [1]success | [2]successful | [3]successfully
  ANSWER(0): succeeded
  EXPLAIN: 复现 ch08 四件套：succeed in doing 固定搭配作谓语动词 → succeeded。success 名词、successful 形容词、successfully 副词，词性都不合。
--- Q18.3: It took great ______ for him to start again at forty.
  OPTS: [0]encourage | [1]courage | [2]discourage | [3]courageous
  ANSWER(1): courage
  EXPLAIN: It takes courage to do sth. 做某事需要勇气——此处需要名词 courage。encourage 是动词「鼓励」，courageous 是形容词「有勇气的」。
--- Q18.4: Your ______ toward the exam decides your result, so stay calm and positive.
  OPTS: [0]attitude | [1]altitude | [2]gratitude | [3]aptitude
  ANSWER(0): attitude
  EXPLAIN: 形近四兄弟：attitude 态度 / altitude 高度 / gratitude 感激 / aptitude 天赋。attitude toward 对……的态度；态度决定结果，本句考 attitude。
--- Q18.5: I wish I ______ as brave as the old keeper when I face the sea.
  OPTS: [0]am | [1]were | [2]will be | [3]have been
  ANSWER(1): were
  EXPLAIN: wish 后的从句与现在事实相反 → 用过去式虚拟，be 动词一律用 were。愿望都是现实里没有的东西：我现在并不 brave，所以用虚拟。
--- Q18.6: It is high time that we ______ a decision about the road ahead.
  OPTS: [0]make | [1]made | [2]will make | [3]have made
  ANSWER(1): made
  EXPLAIN: It is high time that + 过去式（或 should + 原形），表示「该是做……的时候了」，写作模板句。made 是过去式；make 原形是最大陷阱。这句话在第十九章岔路口应验。
--- Q18.7: 改错：He wished that he can sail around the world someday.
  OPTS: [0]can → could | [1]wished → wishes | [2]that → what | [3]sail → sailing
  ANSWER(0): can → could
  EXPLAIN: wish 后的从句用虚拟语气：与将来事实相反用过去式情态动词，can 必须变 could。wished 主句时态没错；that 引导宾语从句不换 what；sail 前有情态动词用原形。
--- Q18.8: 英译汉选择：The old keeper made light of his nineteen medals.
  OPTS: [0]守塔人把十九枚勋章做得很轻。 | [1]守塔人对十九枚勋章毫不在意。 | [2]守塔人点燃了十九枚勋章。 | [3]守塔人把勋章保管得很轻便。
  ANSWER(1): 守塔人对十九枚勋章毫不在意。
  EXPLAIN: make light of 固定搭配「轻视；对……不在乎」，不是把东西做轻或点亮。望文生义是英译汉最大陷阱——老人把勋章锁进抽屉，正是「毫不在意」的剧情呼应。
--- S18.1: The old keeper, who has lived <mark>alone</mark> on the island for thirty years, never feels <mark>lonely</mark> because the sea and the light fill his heart.
  PART: who has lived alone... 非限制性定语从句，补充说明 keeper，逗号不能省
  PART: 现在完成时 + for thirty years：live 是延续性动词，可直接用完成时
  PART: alone 此处作表语性质的状语成分，表示客观独处状态
  PART: lonely 是内心感受；alone but never lonely 正是本句灵魂
--- S18.2: What impressed us most was not the nineteen medals in his drawer, but the <mark>courage</mark> to start again at forty.
  PART: What impressed us most 主语从句：最打动我们的东西
  PART: not...but... 并列结构：不是……而是……（连接两个并列成分）
  PART: to start again at forty 不定式作后置定语，修饰 courage
  PART: 主语从句作主语时谓语用单数：was ✓

########## CH19 岔路口石碑 · 未来抉择 ##########
--- G19.1 t: if 虚拟语气三态（与现在/过去/将来事实相反）
  RULE: 与现在相反：If + 主语 + did/were，主句 would/could/might + do。与过去相反：If + 主语 + had done，主句 would have done。与将来相反（概率极小）：If + 主语 + should/were to do，主句 would + do。口诀：从句时态降一级——现在用 did，过去用 had done，将来用 should/were to；过去虚拟的主句多一个 have done。
  EX: If I were you, I would seize this opportunity. If he had taken the left road last year, he would have missed the best view. If it should rain tomorrow, we would put off the trip.
  EXCN: 如果我是你，我会抓住这个机会。如果去年他走了左边这条路，就会错过最好的风景。万一明天下雨，我们就推迟行程。
  SIGNAL: If I were you 固定用 were 不用 was；改错高频：与过去相反的主句漏掉 have（would miss ❌ → would have missed）；从句有 were/had/should 可省 if 并倒装（Were I you...），选择题认得出即可
--- G19.2 t: suggest / demand / order + (should) do 虚拟
  RULE: suggest（建议）、demand（要求）、order（命令）、insist（坚持要求）、require（要求）、propose（提议）后接 that 从句时，从句谓语用 (should) + 动词原形，should 可省略。两个例外要背：suggest 表「表明/暗示」、insist 表「坚持说（陈述事实）」时，从句用正常陈述语气。
  EX: The spirit demanded that the traveler (should) answer the three questions honestly. Her pale face suggested that she was ill.
  EXCN: 精灵要求旅行者诚实地回答这三个问题。她苍白的脸色表明她生病了。
  SIGNAL: 看到 suggest/demand/order/require + that，直接去从句找 (should) + 原形；改错最爱把原形改成三单或过去式。一词两义是完形常客：suggest 表「表明」时不用虚拟
--- G19.3 t: unless / once / in case 条件句三剑客
  RULE: unless = if not（除非）：Unless you act now, you will regret it。once = 一旦，条件一满足就发生：Once you choose a road, stick to it。in case = 万一、以防（提前做准备）：Take the map in case you get lost；in case of + 名词（in case of fire）。三者从句都用一般现在时表将来，遵循主将从现。
  EX: Unless you make a decision now, you will regret it. Once you choose a road, stick to it. Take the map with you in case you get lost.
  EXCN: 除非你现在就做决定，不然你会后悔。一旦选了一条路，就坚持走下去。带上地图，以防迷路。
  SIGNAL: unless 本身含否定，从句里不许再加 not；in case 后接从句禁用 will（in case it rains ✓）；改错高频：unless 从句误用将来时 will do
--- Q19.1: Don't ______ to ask the spirit if the signpost is unclear.
  OPTS: [0]hesitate | [1]persist | [2]regret | [3]consider
  ANSWER(0): hesitate
  EXPLAIN: hesitate to do 犹豫做——Don't hesitate to do 别客气尽管问，写作万能客套句。persist in、regret doing、consider doing 的接续形式都对不上这里 to do 的坑位。
--- Q19.2: After two more years of study, he finally got his ______ degree.
  OPTS: [0]bachelor's | [1]bachelor | [2]bachelors | [3]bachelors'
  ANSWER(0): bachelor's
  EXPLAIN: 学士学位 = a bachelor's degree，固定说法用所有格 's。master's degree 硕士、doctor's degree 博士同理。
--- Q19.3: The bridge was closed, so we had no ______ but to take the mountain road.
  OPTS: [0]choice | [1]choose | [2]chose | [3]decision
  ANSWER(0): choice
  EXPLAIN: have no choice but to do 别无选择只能做，固定搭配。choose 是动词原形，chose 是过去式，decision 要和 make 搭配（make a decision）。
--- Q19.4: If I ______ you, I would choose the road that scares me more.
  OPTS: [0]am | [1]was | [2]were | [3]be
  ANSWER(2): were
  EXPLAIN: 与现在事实相反的虚拟语气，be 动词一律用 were：If I were you...（本章语法①）。主句 would + do 是另一半信号；was 在正式考试中算错。
--- Q19.5: The professor demanded that every student ______ the form before Friday.
  OPTS: [0]hands in | [1]handed in | [2]hand in | [3]will hand in
  ANSWER(2): hand in
  EXPLAIN: demand + that 从句 → 谓语用 (should) + 动词原形，should 被省略后剩下 hand in（本章语法②）。三单、过去式、将来时都过不了虚拟的安检门。
--- Q19.6: 改错：You will fail the exam unless you will review every day from now on.
  OPTS: [0]will fail → fail | [1]will review → review | [2]unless → if | [3]from → since
  ANSWER(1): will review → review
  EXPLAIN: unless（= if not）引导条件从句，主将从现：主句 will fail ✓，从句必须用一般现在时 review 表将来（本章语法③）。unless 本身含否定，从句里不要再加 not。
--- Q19.7: 英译汉选择：He made a promising start to his career, so don't worry about him.
  OPTS: [0]他给自己的职业生涯许下了一个开始。 | [1]他的职业生涯开局亮眼、前途可期，别为他担心。 | [2]他承诺要开始自己的职业生涯。 | [3]他的职业开始于一个诺言。
  ANSWER(1): 他的职业生涯开局亮眼、前途可期，别为他担心。
  EXPLAIN: promising = 有前途的、大有希望的（promise 的形容词），不是「承诺的」。望文生义是英译汉第一大坑：见到 promise 家族先判词性再定词义。
--- Q19.8: Before making the final decision, you should take all the facts into ______.
  OPTS: [0]count | [1]account | [2]amount | [3]thought
  ANSWER(1): account
  EXPLAIN: take ... into account 把……考虑进去，真题完形高频搭配。account 是 ch01 复现老词：account for 占比例/解释。count in 是另一回事，amount 是数量。
--- S19.1: No matter which road you <mark>choose</mark>, what really matters is not the <mark>decision</mark> itself but the <mark>effort</mark> you make after it.
  PART: No matter which... 让步状语从句：无论选哪条路（= whichever）
  PART: what really matters 主语从句：真正重要的东西
  PART: not...but... 并列表语：不是……而是……
  PART: the effort 后省略了 that 的定语从句 you make after it
--- S19.2: It is widely believed that those who <mark>persist</mark> in their dreams will finally turn every <mark>possibility</mark> into reality.
  PART: It is believed that... 形式主语句：人们普遍认为（写作万能开头）
  PART: who persist in their dreams 定语从句修饰 those；persist in doing 坚持做
  PART: turn...into... 把……变成……；turn A into B
  PART: will finally 一般将来时 + 副词位置：will 后实义动词前

########## CH20 终极考场 · 五关大决战 ##########
--- G20.1 t: Only+状语 倒装 / Never 等否定副词倒装（写作压轴句型）
  RULE: Only + 状语（从句/介词短语/副词）放句首，主句必须部分倒装：把 be/助动词/情态动词提到主语前。Never、Not until、Hardly、By no means 等否定副词放句首，同样倒装。注意：Only 后面的从句本身不倒装，倒装只发生在主句。
  EX: Only when the result came out did he realize how far he had walked. Never before had he felt so calm in an exam hall.
  EXCN: 只有当结果出来时，他才意识到自己走了多远。以前他从没在考场里这么冷静过。
  SIGNAL: 标志：句首 Only when... / Never / Not until → 往后找 be/情态/助动词提前（did he / had he / can you）。改错坑：Only then I knew ❌ → Only then did I know ✓
--- G20.2 t: 强调句 It is/was...that/who
  RULE: 把句子某成分放进 It is/was 和 that 之间加以强调：It was + 被强调部分 + that + 其余部分，强调人可用 who。判断方法：去掉 It is/was...that 框架，剩下的词能还原成完整句子 → 强调句。强调过去的事用 was；谓语动词本身不能被强调。
  EX: It was not the score but the twenty-chapter journey that was the real reward. It was Juanjuan who reminded him to write his name.
  EXCN: 真正的奖励不是分数，而是二十章的旅程。是卷卷提醒他写名字的。
  SIGNAL: 辨析陷阱：It was in the hall where ❌ → that ✓（强调地点也用 that）；It is clear that... 是主语从句不是强调句（去掉框架不成句）
--- G20.3 t: 改错十雷区总复习（终章大阅兵）
  RULE: ①时态：and/but 前后一致，last week 用过去式；②主谓一致：the number of+单数，a number of+复数，either...or 就近；③名词数：one of + 复数；④冠词：a/an 看音素，the same；⑤词性：系动词后用 adj，修饰动词用 adv；⑥代词：主宾格、反身 oneself；⑦非谓语：enjoy+doing，介词后 doing，done 表被动；⑧介词：固定搭配、because of+名词；⑨连词：although 不与 but 连用；⑩平行结构：not only...but also 两边对等。
  EX: Everyone in our class has passed the exam, including the boy who used to get up late.
  EXCN: 我们班每个人都通过了考试，包括那个曾经爱睡懒觉的男孩。
  SIGNAL: 检查口诀：一句读三遍——先看谓语时态和主谓一致，再看名词单复数和冠词，最后查词性、代词、介词、连词、非谓语、平行结构。十雷区逐个点名，一个都不能漏。
--- Q20.1: When the teacher asked him to answer, his mind went completely ______.
  OPTS: [0]blank | [1]blind | [2]empty | [3]white
  ANSWER(0): blank
  EXPLAIN: mind went blank 固定搭配「大脑一片空白」，本章主角词；blind 失明的；empty 强调容器空；white 只说颜色。
--- Q20.2: He received the admission letter and ______ it without hesitation.
  OPTS: [0]accepted | [1]received | [2]refused | [3]avoided
  ANSWER(0): accepted
  EXPLAIN: receive 客观收到（信到手），accept 主观接受（愿意收下）。前半句已用 received，毫不犹豫收下 → accepted；refused 拒绝与语境矛盾。这对老朋友在 ch08/ch18 易混区都练过——终章压轴考点。
--- Q20.3: The high score was the best ______ for his ten months of hard work.
  OPTS: [0]reward | [1]award | [2]price | [3]bargain
  ANSWER(0): reward
  EXPLAIN: reward 回报：努力换来的酬劳，搭配 as a reward for；award 是发给赢家的荣誉奖；price 价格；bargain 便宜货（ch07 回忆杀）。努力对应的是回报。
--- Q20.4: Only when you stay calm in the exam hall ______ your best.
  OPTS: [0]can you do | [1]you can do | [2]did you do | [3]you did do
  ANSWER(0): can you do
  EXPLAIN: Only + 状语从句置于句首 → 主句部分倒装：can you do your best。从句 you stay calm 不倒装；正常语序是 you can do your best。陈述句语序 you can do 是最大陷阱。
--- Q20.5: It was in the exam hall ______ A-Luo met all his old friends again — on paper.
  OPTS: [0]that | [1]where | [2]which | [3]when
  ANSWER(0): that
  EXPLAIN: 强调句：It was + 地点 + that，强调地点也只用 that 不用 where。检验法：去掉 It was...that，剩下 A-Luo met all his old friends in the exam hall 仍是完整句子。
--- Q20.6: 改错：The number of students who take the exam increase every year.
  OPTS: [0]increase → increases | [1]take → takes | [2]students → student | [3]year → years
  ANSWER(0): increase → increases
  EXPLAIN: 雷区②主谓一致：the number of + 复数名词作主语，谓语用单数 → increases；a number of 才配复数谓语。定语从句 who take 修饰 students 用复数没错——别被它骗去改 take。
--- Q20.7: 英译汉选择：Where there is a will, there is a way.
  OPTS: [0]那里有一份遗嘱，那里就有一条路。 | [1]有志者事竟成。 | [2]有意愿的人就有路可走。 | [3]道路在某处，愿望在某处。
  ANSWER(1): 有志者事竟成。
  EXPLAIN: will 在谚语里是名词「意志」，不是将来助动词也不是遗嘱——望文生义是英译汉头号陷阱。这是全书最后一句，也是守塔人和考卷一起送你的话。
--- Q20.8: Please pay attention ______ the instructions before you fill in the blanks.
  OPTS: [0]to | [1]on | [2]at | [3]for
  ANSWER(0): to
  EXPLAIN: pay attention to 固定搭配；同族还有 the answer to / the key to——to 家族是完形改错双高频，改错题最爱把它挖成 on/for。
--- S20.1: What the result tells us is simple: those who stay <mark>calm</mark> in the <mark>exam</mark> hall always perform better than those who let fear take over.
  PART: What the result tells us 主语从句：结果告诉我们的东西
  PART: those who... 定语从句：……的人；who 从句修饰 those
  PART: 比较级结构 perform better than：表现比……更好
  PART: stay calm 系动词 + 形容词；let fear take over 让恐惧接管（使役 + 原形）
--- S20.2: Only when you treat every <mark>blank</mark> as a chance <mark>can you</mark> turn your months of <mark>effort</mark> into a satisfying <mark>grade</mark>.
  PART: Only when... 置于句首，主句用部分倒装：can you turn（本章语法①实弹演示）
  PART: treat A as B：把 A 当作 B
  PART: turn...into... 把……变成……；months of effort 数月的努力

########## CH21 进阶时态 · 时间管理局 ##########
--- G21.1 t: 现在进行时 vs 现在完成进行时（本章第 1 战场）
  RULE: 现在进行时 am/is/are doing：此刻正在发生，看的是「现在这一眼」。现在完成进行时 have/has been doing：从过去某点开始一直持续到现在（还可能继续），看的是「一路走来的时长」。两者都连着现在，区别在于：进行时没有历史，完成进行时自带 history。
  EX: Look! The robot is shouting again. It has been shouting for six hours.
  EXCN: 看！机器人又在喊了。它已经喊了六个小时了。
  SIGNAL: 标志词分工：进行时看 now / at the moment / Look!；完成进行看 since / for / all day。判断标准：动作现在还继续吗？还继续且强调持续多久 → have been doing。改错雷区：has been doing 别丢 been，has doing 是错的
--- G21.2 t: 将来进行时 will be doing vs 将来完成时 will have done（by 一出现就分家）
  RULE: 将来进行时 will be doing：将来某一时刻正在进行，画面感 = 明天这个时候我正在干嘛，标志 at this time tomorrow / this time next week。将来完成时 will have done：到将来某一点为止已经完成，回头看 = 到那时就做完了，标志 by the end of next month / by 2030 / by the time + 现在时从句。
  EX: At this time tomorrow we will be taking the exam. By the end of this term we will have learned 3,000 words.
  EXCN: 明天这个时候我们正在考试。到这学期末，我们将已经学完 3000 个单词。
  SIGNAL: at this time + tomorrow → will be doing；by the end of + 将来时间 → will have done。改错雷区：will be doing 别写成 will being done；will have done 别丢 have
--- G21.3 t: 过去完成进行时 had been doing + 八大时态家族总表
  RULE: 过去完成进行时 had been doing：动作在过去某点之前就开始，一直持续到那个过去时刻（可能还在继续），强调「持续了多久」的过程。必须有过去的参照点（by the time / when + 过去时），for / since 接时长。对比：had done 看结果（先发生的动作），had been doing 看过程（先发生且一直持续的动作）。
  EX: The rescuers arrived at dawn. The old man had been wandering in the forest for two days.
  EXCN: 救援队黎明赶到。老人当时已经在森林里转了两天了。
  SIGNAL: 八大时态家族总表（时态｜结构｜标志词）：一般现在 do/does｜always, usually, every day；现在进行 am/is/are doing｜now, at the moment, Look!；一般过去 did｜yesterday, last week, in 2024, ago；过去进行 was/were doing｜at this time yesterday, when + 过去时；现在完成 have/has done｜since, for, so far, already, yet；过去完成 had done｜by the time, before（过去的过去）；一般将来 will do｜tomorrow, next month, soon, in + 时间段；将来完成 will have done｜by the end of next month。完成进行双补丁：现在完成进行 have been doing（since/for 持续到现在）与过去完成进行 had been doing（过去的过去 + for/since，强调过程）
--- Q21.1: I ______ my notes since seven o'clock, and my hand still hurts now.
  OPTS: [0]reviewed | [1]have reviewed | [2]have been reviewing | [3]was reviewing
  ANSWER(2): have been reviewing
  EXPLAIN: since + 过去时间点，动作持续到现在还在继续 → 现在完成进行时 have been doing，强调「一直做」；have reviewed 只表完成，不强调持续。后半句 and my hand still hurts now 是铁证：动作还连着现在。
--- Q21.2: — Where is Tom? — He ______ the report in the office. He will hand it in ten minutes.
  OPTS: [0]writes | [1]wrote | [2]is writing | [3]has written
  ANSWER(2): is writing
  EXPLAIN: 此刻正在写 → 现在进行时 is writing。后句 will hand it in ten minutes 说明还没交 → 排除 has written（现在完成表已写完）。
--- Q21.3: By the end of this term, we ______ about 3,000 words.
  OPTS: [0]will learn | [1]will have learned | [2]have learned | [3]learned
  ANSWER(1): will have learned
  EXPLAIN: by + 将来时间点 → 将来完成时 will have done，表到将来某点为止已经完成。by the end of this term 就是身份证。
--- Q21.4: The rescuers finally arrived. The old man ______ in the forest for two days.
  OPTS: [0]wandered | [1]was wandering | [2]had been wandering | [3]has been wandering
  ANSWER(2): had been wandering
  EXPLAIN: 以过去动作 arrived 为参照点，之前已持续两天且强调过程 → 过去完成进行时 had been doing，即「过去的过去 + for 时间段」。
--- Q21.5: At this time tomorrow, we ______ the exam in Room 302.
  OPTS: [0]will take | [1]will be taking | [2]will have taken | [3]take
  ANSWER(1): will be taking
  EXPLAIN: at this time tomorrow = 明天此时正在考 → 将来进行时 will be doing。will have taken 是「到时已考完」，时间画面对不上。
--- Q21.6: 下列时态与标志词配对正确的一项是：
  OPTS: [0]so far — 一般过去时 | [1]this time yesterday — 过去进行时 | [2]by the end of next month — 一般现在时 | [3]since two hours ago — 一般将来时
  ANSWER(1): this time yesterday — 过去进行时
  EXPLAIN: this time yesterday 昨天这个时候 → 过去进行时 was/were doing。so far → 现在完成时；by the end of next month → 将来完成时；since + 过去点 → 现在完成时（不是一般将来）。标志词 = 时间的身份证，配错就查无此人。
--- Q21.7: 改错：He has been learning English since he has come to Zhengzhou.
  OPTS: [0]has been learning → has learned | [1]has come → came | [2]since → for | [3]learning → learn
  ANSWER(1): has come → came
  EXPLAIN: 改错雷区①时态：since 引导的时间状语从句用一般过去时 → came。主句的现在完成进行时 has been learning 没错，been 别动。
--- Q21.8: 英译汉选择：By the time you read this letter, I will have been waiting for you for ten years.
  OPTS: [0]当你读这封信的时候，我将一直等你十年。 | [1]等你读到这封信时，我就已经等了你十年了。 | [2]在你读信的时候，我正要等你十年。 | [3]你读这封信之前，我等了你十年。
  ANSWER(1): 等你读到这封信时，我就已经等了你十年了。
  EXPLAIN: by the time + 将来动作 → 将来完成（进行）时 will have been waiting：到那时将已经等了十年。B 选项的「就已经等了十年」才是正确译法；A 把完成时错译成单纯持续，C 时态错乱，D 弄反了时间方向。
--- S21.1: By the end of next term, every student who has been <mark>reviewing</mark> his words day after day <mark>will have built</mark> a vocabulary that no exam can shake.
  PART: By the end of next term 放句首 = 将来完成时的身份证 → 主句用 will have built
  PART: 主语 every student 后接定语从句 who has been reviewing...：现在完成进行，强调从过去一直复习到现在
  PART: day after day 一天又一天 = 持续进行的佐证；a vocabulary 是 will have built 的宾语
  PART: 句尾 that no exam can shake 是定语从句修饰 vocabulary（that 作主语，不能省）
--- S21.2: The file that the Bureau <mark>had been keeping</mark> in the basement proved that the boy who once feared English <mark>had achieved</mark> what many called impossible.
  PART: 主干：The file proved that...；file 后是 that 引导的定语从句（that 作 keeping 的宾语，可省略）
  PART: had been keeping 过去完成进行：在 proved 这个过去动作之前一直在保管，强调持续
  PART: proved 后接 that 宾语从句；从句里 who once feared English 是定语从句修饰 the boy
  PART: had achieved 过去完成：实现发生在 proved 之前的过去；what many called impossible 是 achieved 的宾语从句
